Bid Scanner

Paste a contractor bid. Every line is sorted into the six ADU cost stages, checked against modeled benchmark ranges and verified government fees — and turned into questions to ask before you sign.

Private by design. Everything you paste stays in your browser. This page sends nothing to any server — no account, no email, no upload. The only network requests are to load this site’s own public benchmark and fee tables (two CSV files). Closing the page erases your bid; nothing is saved.

Construction ranges are modeled, not quotes. This scanner flags questions — it does not judge a contractor.

Benchmarks are modeled for an 800 sq ft ADU. The fee check below scales school fees with size; see “How the checks work”.
Verified fees come from the Q3 2026 fee index.

1. Paste your bid

One line per item, with an amount. Lines like Framing labor — $42,000 or $8,500 — Architectural plans both work. Amounts need a $, a comma, or cents (for example 1,234 or 1234.56); a bare number such as 2026 is not read as money. Lines without a readable amount are listed below, never dropped.

        How the checks work

        Classification

        No AI and no outside service. Each line is scored against a keyword list for the six stages (for example permit, plans, engineering point to Soft costs; excavation, concrete, foundation point to Site + foundation). The highest score wins; a line with no keyword match is left Unclassified for you to place. Utility-connection lines (sewer lateral, water service, electrical service) are classified under Systems + interior, and are also tracked separately for the missing-utility check.

        Red-flag rules

        1. Missing stage. A stage with no lines at all is flagged — soft costs and utility connections especially, because they are the two items most often billed separately after signing.
        2. Wording. allowance, TBD, TBC, by owner, owner-supplied, excludes, excluded, not included, and NIC are flagged on the line where they appear (case-insensitive).
        3. Out of range. A stage total below the low end or above the high end of the modeled range for your region is flagged.
        4. Government fees. Lines with fee words (permit, plan check, school fee, impact fee, city/county fee, development fee, inspection fee) are summed. If none appear, that is flagged. If a jurisdiction is selected, the sum is compared with a verified-fee estimate for your ADU size: school fees are $0 under 500 sq ft and the per-sq-ft rate at 500 sq ft or more (Gov. Code §66311.5); impact fees are $0 at 750 sq ft or less and the 751 sq ft cliff amount above that; all other verified fees are taken as-is from the Q3 2026 fee index. A difference larger than $1,000 and larger than half the verified estimate is flagged as “far from verified”.

        What this is not

        The benchmark ranges are modeled from published medians, not quotes and not a price list. A flag means “ask about this”, not “this contractor is wrong”. Site conditions, finish grade, and scope change real prices by tens of thousands of dollars.

        Data

        Benchmark ranges: adu-benchmark-ranges-2026-q3.csv (Q3 2026). Verified fees: adu-fee-index-2026-q3.csv (Q3 2026 fee index, 69 jurisdictions). If either file cannot be loaded, the page falls back to a built-in sample and says so on the page. Jurisdiction links point to that jurisdiction’s verified-fee page under /fees/.
