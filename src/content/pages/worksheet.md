Use the same six rows we use on every build on this site. Drop your contractor's numbers into the blank "Your quote" column and compare them against the modeled medians from the post you're reading.

## Download

**[Download the blank worksheet (CSV)](/downloads/adu-six-phase-worksheet.csv)**

Columns:

| Column | What to put there |
| --- | --- |
| Phase | Soft costs through landscaping, plus Total |
| Your quote ($) | Numbers from your contractor or estimator |
| Our modeled median ($) | Optional — copy from the post, or leave blank |
| Notes | Site conditions, allowances, questions |

The CSV ships blank on purpose. Fill medians from the post you're comparing against.

## Import into Google Sheets

1. Open [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com) and create a blank spreadsheet.
2. **File → Import → Upload** and choose `adu-six-phase-worksheet.csv`.
3. Import location: **Replace spreadsheet** (or insert a new sheet).
4. Separator type: **Comma**.
5. Click **Import data**.

You can then share the sheet with your contractor or keep it private. We don't host a live Google Sheet (that would need Google API credentials we don't have on this site).
