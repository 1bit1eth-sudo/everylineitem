## Download

**[Download the blank worksheet (CSV)](/downloads/adu-six-phase-worksheet.csv)**

Columns:

| Column                 | What to put there                             |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Phase                  | Soft costs through landscaping, plus Total    |
| Your quote ($)         | Numbers from your contractor or estimator     |
| Our modeled median ($) | Optional — copy from the post, or leave blank |
| Notes                  | Site conditions, allowances, questions        |

The CSV ships blank on purpose. Fill medians from the post you're comparing against.

## The six phases, and what goes in each

Every line of a contractor's quote belongs in one of these rows. Sorting the quote yourself is the fastest way to see what it covers and what it leaves out.

| Phase                     | What belongs in it                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Soft costs                | Design, engineering, building permit, plan check, impact and connection fees          |
| Site work and foundation  | Excavation, grading, footings, slab or stem wall, sewer lateral, utility trenching    |
| Framing and structure     | Lumber package, framing labor, trusses, sheathing                                     |
| Building envelope         | Roofing, windows, exterior doors, siding, weather barrier                             |
| Systems and interior      | Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, insulation, drywall, flooring, paint, trim, kitchen, bath |
| Landscaping and hardscape | Paths, stoops, planting, sod, finish grading                                          |

These are the same rows used in the [methodology](/methodology/) and in every cost breakdown on this site.

## Reading your quote against it

- **A row with nothing in it** is a question, not a saving. Ask whether that work is excluded, included somewhere else, or left for you to arrange.
- **Allowances** (a fixed sum for items you have not chosen yet, such as fixtures or flooring) are placeholders. Note them in the Notes column; the final cost moves with what you pick.
- **Government fees** are set by your city, school district and utility, not by the contractor. Check them against your city's [verified fee table](/fees/), and note whether the quote includes them or expects you to pay them directly.
- **"By owner" or "not included" lines** should be listed in the Notes column so they are not forgotten when you compare two bids.

If you would rather not sort the lines by hand, paste the quote into the [Bid Scanner](/bid-scanner/), which sorts it into the same six phases in your browser. (The Bid Scanner files utility-connection lines under systems and interior and also checks for them separately, so a sewer or water line may land in a different row than in this table.)

## Import into Google Sheets

1. Open [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com) and create a blank spreadsheet.
2. **File → Import → Upload** and choose `adu-six-phase-worksheet.csv`.
3. Import location: **Replace spreadsheet** (or insert a new sheet).
4. Separator type: **Comma**.
5. Click **Import data**.

You can then share the sheet with your contractor or keep it private. We don't host a live Google Sheet (that would need Google API credentials we don't have on this site).
