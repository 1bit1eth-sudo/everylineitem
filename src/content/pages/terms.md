## What this site is

Every Line Item publishes line-item cost breakdowns and government fee data for California accessory dwelling units (ADUs) and residential construction. Content is provided for **general information only**.

## Not professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, tax, architectural, engineering, or construction advice, and no figure on this site is a bid, quote, or estimate for your project. Construction costs, permit fees, impact fees, and utility charges vary by parcel and change over time. Always confirm current figures with the relevant city, county, school district, or utility before making financial decisions, and consult licensed professionals for your specific project.

## Accuracy and updates

Fee figures are drawn from official sources (city and county fee schedules, municipal codes, school district resolutions, and utility tariffs) and are re-verified on a published quarterly schedule. Despite that care, sources change, documents are amended, and errors occur. Figures are provided "as is" without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose. Corrections are logged publicly on [Corrections](/corrections/); report an error to [corrections@everylineitem.com](mailto:corrections@everylineitem.com).
