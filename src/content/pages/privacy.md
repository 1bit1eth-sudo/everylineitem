**Last updated:** 8 September 2026

This page explains what Every Line Item (`everylineitem.com`) collects and what we do with it. Short version: we collect as little as we can, we do not sell your information to contractors, and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete what we hold.

## Who we are

Every Line Item publishes line-item cost breakdowns for California ADU and residential construction. Contact for privacy or corrections: [corrections@everylineitem.com](mailto:corrections@everylineitem.com).

## What we collect

### Email (benchmark sheet)

If you subscribe on [Benchmarks](/benchmarks/), we collect the email address you submit so we can send the quarterly benchmark CSV and related updates. Email delivery is handled by **Buttondown**. You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or ask us to remove your address.

The blank [six-phase worksheet](/worksheet/) does **not** require an email.

### Server and hosting logs

The site is hosted on **Cloudflare**. Like most sites, request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps, URLs) may be processed briefly for security, reliability, and abuse prevention. We do not use those logs to build marketing profiles.

### Analytics and cookies

We do not currently run a third-party analytics suite that tracks you across the web for advertising. If we add analytics or display advertising later, this page will be updated before (or when) that happens, and ad/analytics vendors may set cookies as described below.

### Forms and email

Messages you send to `corrections@everylineitem.com` are used only to respond and, if relevant, to update published figures under our [Corrections](/corrections/) policy.

## What we do not do

- **We do not sell your information to contractors.** No lead forms, no “get matched with a builder,” no paid data sharing with builders, lenders, or referral networks.
- We do not sell personal information as that term is commonly used in consumer privacy laws.
- We do not require an account to read the site.

See also [About](/about/) for how the site intends to make money (display ads, software affiliates, or our own paid resources — never contractor lead gen).

## How we use information

- Deliver the benchmark sheet and newsletter-style updates you opted into
- Respond to corrections and support email
- Keep the site secure and working

## Sharing

We share data only with processors that help us run the site (for example Cloudflare for hosting/CDN, Buttondown for email). They are not permitted to sell your email as contractor leads.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the site against abuse.

## Cookies and future advertising

Essential cookies or local storage may be used for basic site functions (for example remembering light/dark theme).

If we enable **display advertising** (as noted on About), ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can limit many ad cookies through your browser settings and industry opt-out tools. We will update this policy when ads go live.

## Your choices

- Unsubscribe from Buttondown emails using the link in any message
- Email [corrections@everylineitem.com](mailto:corrections@everylineitem.com) to ask us to delete your subscriber record or answer privacy questions
- Clear cookies/local storage in your browser for this site

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes to how we use email or ads will be reflected here.
