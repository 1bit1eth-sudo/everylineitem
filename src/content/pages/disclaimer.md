## What this site is

Every Line Item publishes line-item cost breakdowns for California ADU and residential construction. Fee figures come from official government documents linked on each page — a city or county fee schedule, a school district developer-fee resolution, or a utility district charge sheet — with the date we last verified them. Construction cost ranges are modeled from published benchmarks, not quotes from a contractor.

The same six-phase ledger is used across the site so two projects can be compared line by line. How a figure is produced, including assumptions and known limits, is on [Methodology](/methodology/). Tools such as the [worksheet](/worksheet/), [fee index](/fee-index/), and [compare](/compare/) pages apply that same model; they are planning aids, not a bid.

## What this site is not

- **Not a quote.** A modeled range is not a price for your project. Site conditions, design choices, and a contractor's actual bid will move the number. Use the model to see which line on a bid is out of range, then confirm the current fee with the agency that charges it.
- **Not legal, financial, or construction advice.** Fee rules, building codes, and tax treatment change, and they apply differently by parcel. Every Line Item is not a licensed general contractor, architectural firm, municipal permitting agency, or legal or financial advisor. Verify the current figures with the city, the school district, and the utility, and obtain itemized bids from licensed California contractors (CSLB) before you budget or build.
- **Not a guarantee.** We re-verify figures on a rolling basis and log corrections at [Corrections](/corrections/), but a figure can change between our check and your permit application. The "last verified" date on each page is part of the figure. Figures we cannot verify from an official source are marked "verify with city" and excluded from totals.
