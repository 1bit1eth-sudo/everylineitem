## What this site is

Every Line Item publishes line-item cost breakdowns for California ADU and residential construction. Fee figures come from official government documents linked on each page; construction cost ranges are modeled from published benchmarks, not quotes from a contractor.

## What this site is not

- **Not a quote.** A modeled range is not a price for your project. Site conditions, design choices, and a contractor's actual bid will move the number.
- **Not legal, financial, or construction advice.** Fee rules, building codes, and tax treatment change, and they apply differently by parcel. Verify the current figures with the city, the school district, and the utility before you budget or build.
- **Not a guarantee.** We re-verify figures on a rolling basis and log corrections at [Corrections](/corrections/), but a figure can change between our check and your permit application. The "last verified" date on each page shows when we last confirmed it.

## Advertising and affiliates

The site is configured for display advertising through Google AdSense. Any affiliate relationship will be disclosed on the page where it appears. Advertising and affiliate content never changes which fee figures we publish.
