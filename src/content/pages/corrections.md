Cost figures age, jurisdictions change their fees, and we make mistakes.

**When a number on this site is wrong, we change it and we log the change
here.** We don't quietly edit and move on. The original figure, the new
figure, and the reason all stay visible.

## Why we do this

Anyone can publish a cost estimate. What separates a useful cost estimate
from a guess is whether the person publishing it will stand behind it in
public when it turns out to be wrong.

A correction log is the cheapest possible proof that we will.

## What counts as a correction

| Type | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| A figure was wrong | Logged below with old value, new value, and reason |
| A source changed (fee schedule, state law) | Logged below, post updated, "last verified" date advanced |
| Routine quarterly re-verification, no change | "Last verified" date advanced, not logged |
| Typo, formatting, added link | Not logged |

## Report an error

If a figure here doesn't match your local permit records, your actual quote,
or a source you can point to — tell us. Include the post, the figure, and
what you're seeing instead.

[corrections@everylineitem.com](mailto:corrections@everylineitem.com)

The most valuable messages we get are from contractors and estimators
telling us where the model breaks. We'd rather find that out at build ten
than at build fifty.
