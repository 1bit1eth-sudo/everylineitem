## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, so San Fernando's $4,304 does not change under SB 1117.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The official city fee schedule.** The first document to find. It determines the permit, plan check, impact, and utility lines — the entire city stack, currently $0 by omission rather than by policy.
2. **The schedule link on file.** It currently points to a zoning code chapter, not a fee table. Correcting that pointer is a data-quality fix for the next verification round.
3. **The primary dwelling's size.** Once city impact rates are known, the 750 sq ft proration — and from 2027, the SB 1117 above-750-only rule — makes the main house's square footage a direct input to the ADU's fee.
