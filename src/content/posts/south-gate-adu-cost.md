South Gate's floor is two lines: a school fee and a zero. The LAUSD school fee is **$4,304** at the district's documented **$5.38 per square foot**, and the water/sewer connection and capacity line is **$0** under the city's Ordinance 2020-01. Everything else the city charges is still unknown, because the document that would price it — the current building permit valuation table — did not surface this round. The FY 2026 planning fee schedule was located, but it prices planning applications, not building permits, and the older 2021-22 schedule's $142-per-square-foot valuation is too stale to compute from.

The $0 utility line deserves a precise reading. It is a one-time connection and capacity figure, not a promise about monthly bills. An ADU in South Gate still consumes water and sewer service; what the ordinance zeroes is the connection/capacity charge at permit time. That distinction matters because other cities in this series charge thousands for the same connection — South Gate's zero is a genuine, documented saving on the one-time side, and it is the reason this post counts two lines instead of one.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. South Gate's $0 connection line is therefore unaffected by SB 1117 either way. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, so South Gate's $4,304 does not change under SB 1117.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The building permit valuation table.** The single biggest unknown. Once the current valuation rate and permit table are located, the permit stack can be computed the way Sunnyvale's was in this batch.
2. **City impact-fee treatment for ADUs.** Park, Quimby, and any other development impact fees are unverified for ADUs in South Gate. Their rates and their proration basis decide the next-largest line.
3. **Planning fees.** The FY 2026 planning schedule exists; whether any planning application fee applies to a ministerial ADU was not confirmed. Most likely $0 for a standard ADU, but not verified.

## How South Gate compares

Only published posts are used here, and South Gate's floor counts fewer lines than any of them — so read this as a gap in the data, not proof South Gate is cheaper. Published Los Angeles County totals: Pasadena **~$310,000**, Downey **$311,000**, Long Beach **$313,000**, Torrance **$317,000**, Glendale **$318,000**, Burbank **$320,000**. South Gate's $306,304 sits below all of them while missing the permit and impact lines entirely; once those are counted, expect it to land inside that band. Its documented $0 utility connection is a real edge over cities that charge for the same hookup — but it is not enough, on its own, to keep South Gate below the band.
