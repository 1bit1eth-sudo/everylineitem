> **Published:** 11 Dec 2026 · **Last verified:** 30 Oct 2026 · **Next review:** Jan 2027

Milpitas does not charge a flat building fee for ADUs. Its adopted Schedule of User and Regulatory Fees, effective **November 2, 2026**, prices plan review and inspection on a tiered table: each size tier carries a base fee, plus a charge for every additional 100 square feet. If you are budgeting a Milpitas ADU, this table — not a single number — is the document.

## The table

## How to read it

Find the largest tier at or below your ADU's size, take that tier's base fees, then add the tier's per-100-square-foot increments for each 100 sq ft above the tier. Two worked examples, calculated from the table:

**800 sq ft ADU.** Start at the 500 SF tier: $1,559 plan review + $1,944 inspection = $3,503. Add three 100-sq-ft increments: 3 × $166.20 = $498.60 plan review, 3 × $194.40 = $583.20 inspection. **Total: $4,584.80.**

**1,200 sq ft ADU.** Start at the 1,000 SF tier: $2,390 + $2,916 = $5,306. Add two increments: 2 × $221.60 = $443.20 plan review, 2 × $194.40 = $388.80 inspection. **Total: $6,138.00.**

Small ADUs sit on the lower tiers with no increment column: a 200 sq ft unit is $728 + $729 = **$1,457**; a 400 sq ft unit is $1,282 + $1,458 = **$2,740**.

## Garage, conversion, and tract rules

- Detached garage / accessory structure: **$1,457**
- Garage conversion: **$1,371**
- Tract or repetitive projects: plan review at **25% of the original** fee, inspection the same as regular. The schedule's inflator column is marked Y for the standard rows and N for tract/repetitive.

Two footnotes matter for budgeting. First, the city's standard issuance fees and state-imposed surcharges are **separate** from the figures above. Second, additional hourly charges may apply. Neither is quantified in the schedule row, so the totals above are the table's fees, not an all-in permit bill.

Our fee calculator does not yet model tiered schedules like this one — it handles flat, per-square-foot, and valuation-based fees — so the Milpitas fee page links this schedule rather than computing from it. Use the table above for Milpitas building fees until tiered calculation is added.
