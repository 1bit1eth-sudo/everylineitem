Pacific Grove's $306,487 rests on two verified lines and one confirmed zero. The school fee is $4,136 at the district's $5.17 rate, the sewer lateral report review is $351 in the current fiscal year's schedule, and planning approval for a compliant ADU costs $0 because the municipal code requires none. The building permit — usually the largest fee line — is the blank that matters most.

The school fee carries a caveat worth stating plainly. The district's own March 2024 hearing notice proposed adopting the January 2024 State Allocation Board Level I rate, which is $5.17 per square foot, and the May 9, 2024 board meeting adopted the increase. That supports $5.17 as the collected rate. But the adoption resolution itself was not read in this round, and no 2026 adoption of the $5.38 rate was found — so the draft counts $4,136 and flags +$168 as the open variant rather than silently assuming the 2026 rate.

The $351 sewer lateral report review is the cleanest line in the post: it appears in the FY 2026-27 Master Fee Schedule alongside its FY 2025-26 figure of $334, so both the amount and its year-over-year movement are visible. Do not mistake it for a connection charge — it pays for review of the lateral report, and connection and capacity charges remain uncounted.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: no per-square-foot impact fee is counted, and the school fee is untouched by SB 1117.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The 2026 school rate.** If Pacific Grove Unified adopted the $5.38 SAB rate, the floor rises $168 to $306,655. Reading the district's 2026 resolution settles it.
2. **The building permit.** The valuation-based schedule was not computed to a figure. Running it for an 800 sq ft ADU would convert the largest missing line into a countable one.
3. **Sewer and water connections.** The $351 review fee is not a connection charge. The serving utilities' connection and capacity charges are entirely uncounted.
