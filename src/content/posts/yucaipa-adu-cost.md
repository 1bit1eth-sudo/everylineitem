Yucaipa's $302,200 is the thinnest total in this batch — and that thinness is the point. One $200 water district fee sits on top of the series construction baseline, while the school fee, the city impact fee, and the water capacity charge are all blank for documented reasons. Treat this post as a starting line, not a budget.

The one verified line is the **Yucaipa Valley Water District administrative processing fee of $200 per ADU**, from Resolution No. 2026-32, adopted April 21, 2026, revising the district's ADU policies. The fee is nonrefundable, due when application materials are submitted, and includes a fire flow test. The same resolution is more interesting for what it prices but does not yet let us count: Facility Capacity Charges for an ADU are proportional by size, and an 800 sq ft ADU falls in the 750–874 square foot tier at **25% of current capacity charges**. Without the base capacity charge amounts, 25% of an unknown is still unknown — so the capacity line stays out of the total, flagged rather than estimated.

The school fee has two near-misses, neither countable. The district's FY 2024/2025 annual report lists $3.79 per square foot residential — but with an effective window of July 1 to July 13, 2024, a thirteen-day window that signals a superseded rate, not a current one. And the district's CFD No. 1 Tax B of $5.18 per square foot is a special tax adopted for FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27 — not a Level I fee, and applicable only inside CFD boundaries that were not verified for any parcel, with ADU treatment unconfirmed. Counting either as "the school fee" would misstate what it is.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the $200 fee is an administrative processing fee, not an impact fee, and the YVWD capacity charges are capacity charges in the excluded category.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The YVWD capacity charge base.** The 25% tier for an 800 sq ft ADU is confirmed; the base amounts it applies to are not. Extracting the district's current Facility Capacity Charge schedule converts this tier into the largest water-side line.
2. **The YCJUSD current Level I rate.** The successor to the thirteen-day $3.79 window is the school line's missing piece; a 2025 or 2026 district adoption would add several thousand dollars.
3. **The city impact fee schedule.** The program exists; the current rate table does not surface. Once it does, the city impact line — and its SB 1117 proration — can be computed.
