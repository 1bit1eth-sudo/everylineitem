## Three fees homeowners most often get wrong

**1. Treating the full-unit baselines as ADU prices.** Clovis publishes a thorough Master Development Fee Schedule — parks at $6,147 per unit, sewer major facilities at $9,661 per unit, water major facilities up to $13,795 per unit — and every one of those numbers is a new-home figure. None of them is labeled as an ADU amount, and the city does not say which lines are prorated for ADUs. Summing them into an ADU budget produces a five-figure error in the wrong direction. They are useful only as the inputs a prorated calculation would start from, once the city confirms the method.

**2. Picking one school-fee answer and budgeting it.** The district page is genuinely ambiguous, and the ambiguity is worth $800. Level 1 ($5.38) is labeled for additions and remodels; Level 2 ($6.38) for new construction including multi-family; ADUs over 500 sq ft are assessed consistently with new residential construction on the entire square footage, in the district page's wording. The reading most consistent with that wording is Level 2 — $5,104 — but the page never says so outright, and publishing $5,104 as confirmed would overstate what the document proves. The floor uses Level 1 ($4,136) and flags the $800 uplift, rather than silently choosing. One more point the page does settle: the fee applies to the full 800 sq ft, not just the 300 sq ft above 500.

**3. Assuming the 749 plan solves the 800 sq ft problem.** The pre-approved program's plan numbered 749 is a deliberate design: one square foot under the impact-fee cliff, it keeps a project exempt from city impact fees entirely. An 800 sq ft ADU cannot use that exemption by relabeling — it is over the line, and it pays proportional impact fees. The program is still valuable at 800 sq ft (pre-approval confirms zoning, building, and safety compliance), but it does not move the cliff. And the free Cottage Home plans — three designs, no charge — are under 450 sq ft and require alley access; they are a different program for a different project.

## The ordinance, in brief

The city's handout (August 2023) sets the shape of a Clovis ADU: detached units up to 1,200 sq ft, 4 ft rear and side setbacks, 16 ft height for detached units, JADUs up to 500 sq ft inside the primary dwelling with owner-occupancy required for the JADU only. No deed restriction and no owner-occupancy requirement apply to a full ADU. An 800 sq ft detached ADU fits comfortably inside every one of those limits — the constraints on this project are financial, not dimensional.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 1, 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Clovis's proportional formula cannot survive that math unchanged — an 800 sq ft ADU would owe fees on 50 sq ft of proportional impact, not 800, shrinking the cliff penalty to a sliver of its current size. School fees are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition: the Level 1 vs. Level 2 question is untouched by it, and the 500 sq ft school-fee exemption stands. The January 2027 quarterly review will check for a city-published ADU proration method and a district-confirmed school-fee level.
