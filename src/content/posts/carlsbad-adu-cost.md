**DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.** Numbers below are verified against City of Carlsbad and Carlsbad Unified documents as of October 25, 2026, unless marked otherwise.

Carlsbad impact fees 2026 are the rare case where the cheapest line in the budget is also the best documented. The city gives away the plans, states the permit range in plain dollars, and its school district posts a board-adopted rate that matches the state maximum to the cent. An 800 sq ft detached ADU here lands at **$294,000, or $368 per square foot** — a floor, not a total, and the lowest floor in this batch. It counts the series-baseline construction phases, the $2,000 low end of the city's permit range, and a $4,304 school fee. Design is $0 because the plans are free. Park impact fees are excluded because the schedule does not state that its park table applies to ADU lots.

One line is deliberately absent. A verified floor total for a 2,000 sq ft single-family new construction cannot be stated from official sources: fee schedules price permits and fees, not construction, so no such figure is verified here and none is estimated. The 2,000 sq ft figure does matter in one place — proportionality. An 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling pays 40% of any applicable full impact fee, not the full fee.

> **The ADU impact-fee box.** California law bars any impact fee on an ADU under 750 square feet, and requires proportional charges at 750 square feet or more (Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A)). Carlsbad's bulletin applies the same rule. The practical consequence for an 800 sq ft ADU: multiply the applicable full fee by the ADU's share of the primary dwelling — 40% behind a 2,000 sq ft house, 80% behind a 1,000 sq ft house. The full fee itself still has to come from the city, because the published park table does not say it covers ADUs.

## Three things that can still move the number

**1. The park table's fine print.** The FY 2026-27 schedule's park-district figures are real numbers — $6,020 for a residential unit under 800 sq ft, $9,850 for 800–1,300 sq ft — but the table is presented for residential subdivisions. Nothing in the material found states that an ADU lot pays these figures, proportionally or otherwise. Adding $9,850, or 40% of it, to an ADU budget would be guessing. It stays out of the total until the city confirms ADU treatment.

**2. The permit range's vintage.** The $2,000–$4,000 range is the city's own statement, but it cites the FY 2024-25 schedule while a FY 2026-27 schedule has since taken effect (September 1, 2026). The range may still hold, or the new schedule may have moved it. The floor uses the low end and flags the gap rather than presenting the range as current-year verified.

**3. The district boundary.** The $4,304 school fee is verified for Carlsbad Unified parcels. Carlsbad addresses near the city's edges can fall in other districts with different adopted rates. The district page itself ties its rates to its own boundaries. Confirm the serving district before carrying $4,304 into a budget.
