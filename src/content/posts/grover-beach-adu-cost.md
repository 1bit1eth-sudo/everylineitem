Grover Beach prices an 800 sq ft ADU's building permit and plan check as a single flat line. The Master Fee Schedule (FY2026-27, effective July 1, 2026) puts the R3 single-family/duplex/ADU row at **$2,984** for an 800 sq ft unit — permit inspection and plan review in one number, no per-square-foot math, no separate plan-check percentage. The water and sewer connection is **$0** because the city's ADU ordinance does not require separate utility connections or meters for an ADU on an existing service. And the Quimby park fee is **$0** because it is collected only at subdivision. Our six-phase total lands at **$305,000, or $381 per square foot** — tied for the lowest total in this series so far — but it is a floor, not a finished figure. The school district fee is not yet verified, so it is not in the total at all.

The rest of the stack is just as flat. Planning review of the building plans adds **$340** and permit processing **$92** — both flat lines from the same schedule — for a verified fee stack of **$3,416**. The only verified impact line, Quimby, is $0 for an infill ADU. What is not in the total is the school fee — no verified school-fee row exists for Grover Beach, so it is excluded rather than guessed at. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Grover Beach does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## One flat line, two zeros

The $2,984 permit is the quirk, and it is worth naming precisely because it is one line instead of a stack. Most cities in this series price the permit, the plan check, and the processing as separate lines with separate bases; Grover Beach's R3 row bundles permit inspection and plan review into a single flat figure for an 800 sq ft ADU. The $340 planning review and the $92 processing fee sit outside that bundle, but they are flat too — the whole verified stack is five lines, three of them priced, two of them $0.

The two zeros are different kinds of zero. The water and sewer connection is $0 by ordinance: the city's ADU code says separate connections and meters are not required, so an ADU sharing the existing service owes nothing for the connection. The Quimby fee is $0 by timing: it is a subdivision exaction collected at map, so a backyard ADU on an existing lot never triggers it. Neither zero is a waiver that could be withdrawn at permit time; both are how the fee is structured.

## One gap that matters

This total is a floor, and the gap is the school fee. We have no verified school district fee for Grover Beach — the district's rate is not in our data, and at the $5.38 state maximum it would add $4,304, taking the total to about $309,000. That is the single largest known adjustment, and it is a real cost a homeowner will pay.

One more limit is worth stating plainly. Quimby is the only impact-fee line on file for Grover Beach, and it is $0. No other impact-fee category has a verified row — so this post does not claim every impact fee a Grover Beach ADU could face is $0, only that the one verified line is. When other impact lines are verified, this post will be updated.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the only verified impact line (Quimby) is already $0 for an infill ADU, and the school fee is untouched by SB 1117.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Grover Beach's only verified impact line — Quimby — is already $0 for an infill ADU, so SB 1117 changes nothing we can compute here — there is nothing to reduce. See the full comparison at [Grover Beach: 2026 vs 2027, line by line](/sb-1117/grover-beach/). School fees, permit fees and water/sewer charges are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and do not change.
