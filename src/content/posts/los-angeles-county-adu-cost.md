## City of LA vs unincorporated County

"Los Angeles County" is not one counter.

| Where the parcel sits | Typical permit path | What to open first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **City of Los Angeles** | [LADBS](https://www.ladbs.org/) plan check / permits | [LADBS Permit & Inspection Report](https://www.ladbsservices2.lacity.org/onlineservices/?service=plr); zoning via [ZIMAS](https://zimas.lacity.org/) |
| **Unincorporated LA County** (and many contract cities on the county system) | [EPIC-LA](https://epicla.lacounty.gov/) | EPIC-LA portal; [LA County Open Data — EPIC-LA Permit Finder](https://data.lacounty.gov/datasets/epic-la-permit-finder) |
| Other incorporated cities inside the county | That city's building department (sometimes still EPIC-LA) | Local fee schedule PDF + that city's permit portal |

This matters for **schedule, fee worksheets, and which declared valuations you can search** — not because this hub publishes a second set of City-only or County-only totals. The series medians remain the LA County mid-grade scenarios above. How those figures are anchored to public permit records is spelled out on [Methodology](/methodology/).

Practical difference for a homeowner:

1. Confirm jurisdiction (city vs unincorporated) before you download a fee schedule.
2. Search recent ADU permits on the portal that actually covers your address.
3. Treat declared construction valuation as a **floor**, not the contract price — same caveat as Methodology.

## Soft costs & the 750 sq ft impact-fee cliff

Across the series, soft costs for typical Los Angeles County mid-grade detached ADUs land in a **$28,000–$40,000** band. Published model points:

- 400 detached: **$28,000** (13% of $215,000)
- 800 detached: **$32,000** (10% of $320,000)
- Garage conversion: **$24,000** (13% of $190,000)

Doubling floor area from 400 to 800 only adds about **$4,000** in soft costs in these medians — design and plan check dominate more than square footage. Line-item ranges and jurisdiction notes: [ADU soft costs](/posts/adu-soft-costs-permits-california/).

### The 750 sq ft cliff (state law)

Under [California Government Code §66311.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?chapter=13&division=1.&lawCode=GOV&part=&title=7.) (see also the [HCD ADU Handbook](https://www.hcd.ca.gov/building-standards/adu/handbook) and [Methodology](/methodology/)):

- An ADU with **750 sq ft of interior livable space or less** generally cannot be charged **impact fees** by a local agency, special district, or water corporation.
- Above **750 sq ft**, any such impact fees must be charged **proportionately** relative to the primary dwelling — not as a full single-family fee.
- **Impact fee ≠ connection fee or capacity charge.** Water/sewer connection and capacity charges can still apply under 750 sq ft.
- **School developer fees** follow **Education Code** rules with a separate size threshold (commonly discussed around **500 sq ft**). Do not assume the 750 rule covers schools.

In this series, the **400** model sits below the cliff; the **800** model sits **50 sq ft above** it. In the soft-costs illustrative splits, impact / ADU / school fees only move about **$2,000 → $3,000** between those models — a small step in the LA County mid-grade medians because design and plan check dominate phase one and many local ADU impact schedules were already compressed. In cities that still levy large proportional impact fees above 750 sq ft, the soft-cost jump can be larger than these medians imply. Always read the current municipal fee schedule PDF.

## What moves the median

The same drivers appear across the series. None of these invent a new county total; they explain why a quote can sit above or below the published medians.

1. **Site access and utility distance.** Long trench runs, panel upgrades, and awkward crane access move site work hard. The 800 post cites roughly **$18,000** in Los Angeles County for a representative utility-distance package — and that line barely shrinks when the unit is smaller.
2. **Hillside / slope.** Engineered footings, retaining, and access can add on the order of **$20,000–$50,000** without changing the floor plan (same framing used in the 800 post).
3. **Coastal vs inland band.** Same 800 sq ft drawings in this series: Inland Empire near **$300,000 ($375 / sq ft)**, LA County median **$320,000 ($400 / sq ft)**, LA coastal band near **$380,000 ($475 / sq ft)**. Full table: [Coastal vs inland](/posts/coastal-vs-inland-adu-cost-california/).
4. **Finish grade.** Builder-grade vs mid-grade across a full unit is typically **$15,000–$30,000** in the series framing — one of the few levers you still control after permits.
5. **Modular vs site-built.** Factory-built paths in this series have come in about **10%–25%** below comparable site-built projects, with crane/transport offsets. See [Modular vs site-built](/posts/modular-vs-site-built-adu-cost-california/).
6. **Jurisdiction fee path.** City of LA (LADBS) vs unincorporated County (EPIC-LA) vs other cities — fee schedules and review tracks differ even when state ADU law is the same. Soft-cost deep dive: [permits and soft costs](/posts/adu-soft-costs-permits-california/).

## How to verify on your parcel

Do this in order — same discipline as Methodology and the soft-costs post:

1. Confirm **city vs unincorporated** (and which portal applies).
2. Download the **current building permit fee schedule** and any ADU-specific handout for that agency.
3. Cross-check size / fee rules against the **[HCD ADU Handbook](https://www.hcd.ca.gov/building-standards/adu/handbook)** and **Gov. Code §66311.5** for the 750 sq ft impact-fee line.
4. Open **[EPIC-LA](https://epicla.lacounty.gov/)** or the **[LADBS Permit & Inspection Report](https://www.ladbsservices2.lacity.org/onlineservices/?service=plr)** and search recent ADU permits near your address; note declared valuations.
5. For City of LA parcels, check zoning / overlays in **[ZIMAS](https://zimas.lacity.org/)**.
6. Ask the counter (or your architect) for a **written fee estimate** before you lock a construction budget.
7. Drop any contractor quote into the blank six-phase rows and compare to the series medians → [Worksheet](/worksheet/) and [Compare](/compare/).

Declared valuations understate contract price. Use them as a floor and a relative signal — not as your all-in number. Broader owning costs: [All-in cost](/posts/adu-all-in-cost-california/). Rent math: [Rent payback / ROI](/posts/adu-rent-payback-roi-california/).
