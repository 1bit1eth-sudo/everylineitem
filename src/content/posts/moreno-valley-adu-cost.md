Moreno Valley is the first city in this series where the two most common cost drivers both point the same way. The development impact fee is **$0 for an ADU** — the adopted schedule lists it as Exempt, where a single-family home pays $10,104. And the city hands you the plans for free: seven city-owned pre-approved detached ADU designs, from a 306 sq ft studio to a 988 sq ft three-bedroom, under the "ADU for You" program launched in December 2024. The permit package is **$3,746 all-in** — plan check $1,385, inspection $2,310, filing $51 — from a dedicated ADU category in the FY 2026-27 fee schedule. Our six-phase total lands at **$310,000, or $388 per square foot**.

The rest of the ledger is just as clean. The school fee is the state maximum $5.38 per square foot from Moreno Valley Unified, $4,304 — effective June 29, 2026. Planning is $0: ADUs are processed ministerially, straight to Building. What is not in the total is the water and sewer side — no verified connection or capacity row exists for an ADU, so those charges are excluded rather than guessed at. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Moreno Valley does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## Seven free plans, and what they do not cover

The "ADU for You" program is the most generous design offer in this series so far: seven free, city-owned, pre-approved detached ADU plans, each with multiple architectural styles, from a 306 sq ft studio to a 988 sq ft three-bedroom. The plans have already been reviewed by Building & Safety, which can speed up permitting. To qualify, the lot must be zoned residential with an existing single-family home or duplex, and it cannot sit in a geohazard, landslide, flood or Wildland-Urban Interface zone.

What the free plans do not do is erase the design line. A homeowner still needs a site plan drawn for the lot, and site-specific engineering and Title 24 work still apply. So this ledger keeps the $14,000 design and engineering baseline in, and treats the free plans as upside: use one, and the real bill comes in under the $310,000 shown here. That is the honest way to carry a free plan in a ledger — as a saving you can actually claim, not as a number we subtracted on your behalf.

## A $0 impact fee, with one footnote

The $0 impact fee deserves one careful footnote. The adopted DIF schedule (effective July 1, 2023) lists the City DIF as Exempt for an ADU — that is the governing document, and it is what this ledger uses. But an older city ADU handout says the DIF applies at 750 sq ft and above, which is the opposite reading. The adopted schedule governs over the handout, and the discrepancy is flagged for a cross-check on December 1. If the schedule reading ever changes, this post will be corrected.

Two smaller footnotes sit on the school fee. The $5.38 rate is the Moreno Valley Unified rate; slices of the city in the southwest fall in Val Verde Unified, whose rate is unverified — a homeowner there should confirm which district covers the lot. And lots in a Community Facilities District pay higher CFD rates instead of the standard schedule. Neither changes the $4,304 in this ledger, which uses the MVUSD rate for the standard case.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Moreno Valley's impact fee is already $0, so SB 1117 changes nothing we can compute here — there is nothing to reduce. See the full comparison at [Moreno Valley: 2026 vs 2027, line by line](/sb-1117/moreno-valley/). School fees, permit fees and water/sewer charges are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and do not change.
