> **DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.**

An 800 sq ft detached ADU in Fountain Valley costs at least **$305,301, or about $382 per square foot**, on verified figures alone — and anyone searching for Fountain Valley ADU fees will find that the city's own schedule never names an ADU at all. There is no ADU line, no ADU waiver, and no ADU surcharge in the FY 2026-27 schedule. What there is, instead, is a valuation table and a set of percentages, and this post is the arithmetic of applying them to an 800 sq ft unit. That is both the strength and the limit of the number: every dollar in it can be traced to a table cell, and every dollar that cannot be traced that way is left out.

## The valuation table is the permit

Most cities in this series price an ADU permit as a line item. Fountain Valley prices it as a consequence of a valuation. The FY 2026-27 schedule, adopted under Resolution No. 9978 and effective July 6, 2026, states that project valuation follows the most recent ICC Building Valuation Table and reproduces the February 2026 table. It does not state a construction type for ADUs, so this post uses Group R-3, Type VB — wood-frame construction, the typical detached ADU — and flags the alternative openly:

| Building line (FY 2026-27, 800 sq ft) | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Valuation: 800 sq ft × $175.92 (R-3, Type VB) | $140,736 |
| Building plan check (65% of inspection fee) | $1,126.22 |
| Building permit / inspection fee | $1,732.65 |
| Permit issuance | $21.30 |
| **Building subtotal, counted** | **$2,880.17** |
| General Plan Maintenance (10% of permit fee) | $173.27 |
| Planning Plan Check (22% of plan check) | $247.77 |
| **Planning subtotal, counted** | **$421.04** |
| **City total, counted** | **$3,301.21** |

The prior year's same-scope total was $3,142.65, so the year-on-year increase is $158.56 — but the composition moved in opposite directions: the building lines rose with the new valuation table while the planning subtotal fell, because the prior year's $28.41 SMIP charge has no line in the current schedule and is not carried forward. A reader comparing the two years without that note would misread the planning line as a cut. It is not a cut; it is a missing line, and the city should confirm whether a state strong-motion charge is still collected at the counter.

Sensitivity, stated rather than buried: if the city classes the unit as Type VA ($187.70 per square foot), the valuation becomes $150,160, the building subtotal becomes $3,031.15, the planning add-ons become $443.27, and the city total becomes $3,474.42 — $173.21 higher. Confirm the construction type with the city before treating $3,301.21 as a quote.

## The full line-item breakdown

Six phases, the same six used on every build in this series. Our method is described in full on the [methodology](/methodology/) page; in short, phases 2 through 6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline because Fountain Valley does not publish phase-level construction costs, while phase 1 is built from Fountain Valley documents wherever possible.

| Phase | Cost | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft costs (design, permits, fees) | $17,301 | 6% |
| Site work + foundation | $38,000 | 12% |
| Framing + structure | $48,000 | 16% |
| Building envelope | $42,000 | 14% |
| Systems + interior | **$147,000** | 48% |
| Landscaping & hardscape | $13,000 | 4% |
| **Total** | **$305,301** | **$382 / sq ft** |

The soft-costs phase is $14,000 of design from the series baseline plus the $3,301.21 city total. Everything else that usually lives in that phase — the school fee, the impact fees, the trade fees, and the utility charges — is excluded, not estimated. A floor that quietly absorbed guesses would stop being a floor.

> **The 750 sq ft rule, in plain terms.** California's ADU fee statute, Government Code section 65852.2(f)(3)(A), bars any impact fee on an ADU smaller than 750 square feet, and requires that an ADU of 750 square feet or more be charged only in proportion to the primary dwelling's square footage. Fountain Valley's per-unit traffic and park figures are therefore not ADU figures at all until they are scaled by a real primary dwelling — which is why they sit in the reference column here. From January 1, 2027, SB 1117 (Government Code section 66311.5) changes the treatment for lots with two or fewer ADUs: only the area in excess of 750 square feet is charged, so an 800 sq ft unit would be charged on 50 sq ft. That future rule is not in this post's 2026 total, and it does not change the school fee, which is not a covered impact fee. For the full explanation of how the cliff works, see [The 750 sq ft cliff](/posts/750-sq-ft-cliff-explained/).

## Three things that can still move the number

**1. The construction type.** Type VB versus Type VA moves the city total by $173.21 before any other line changes. It is the single cheapest question in this post to answer and the one most likely to be wrong if assumed: ask the city which type it applies to a detached wood-frame ADU, and re-run the band before quoting $3,301.21.

**2. The school fee and the impact-fee proportions.** Together these are the largest uncounted block: $4,304 on the school line if charged on the full area, plus traffic and park fees that scale with the primary dwelling. None of them can be fixed without the parcel's feeder district and the primary home's size. [ADU school fees, explained](/posts/adu-school-fees-explained/) covers why a statewide cap cannot stand in for a district rate.

**3. The counter charges.** SMIP, trade fees, the fire sprinkler fee, and utility connections are all priced in the schedule as unit or project-specific lines, and none of them is in the floor. When the city answers on SMIP and the trade scope, those lines join the floor by addition.

## Fountain Valley vs. Los Angeles County

The series baseline is shared, so the comparison isolates local fees rather than construction costs. Our published LA County cities price the same 800 sq ft detached ADU between $313,000 and $320,000, with the spread driven by permits, impact fees, school fees, and utility charges rather than by framing or finishes.

| 800 sq ft detached ADU | Total | What drives the difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fountain Valley (verified floor) | **$305,301** | $3,301.21 city permit and planning total only; school, impact, trade, and utility lines excluded |
| Long Beach (LA County) | $313,000 | Reduced ADU impact-fee rates; modest permit fees |
| Burbank (LA County) | $320,000 | Highest permit-plus-plan-check in the LA County set |

Fountain Valley sits below the LA County range, but the comparison is deliberately uneven: Fountain Valley's figure is missing its school fee, its impact fees, and every trade and utility line, while the LA County figures include theirs. Add only the school reference figure and Fountain Valley reaches $309,605 — still below the range, because the impact and trade lines are still missing. The honest reading is not that Fountain Valley is cheap; it is that Fountain Valley's countable permit bill is small and its parcel-specific fees are where the money is.

How to use this floor in practice: treat $305,301 as the number below which the project should not be budgeted, confirm the construction type and the SMIP position with the city, pin the parcel's school feeder and the primary dwelling's size, and carry separate, explicit allowances for the school fee, the traffic and park fees, the trade fees, and the utility charges. When the city and district answer, the floor converts into a budget by addition, not by revision. That is the point of keeping guesses out of the total in the first place.

## What this post does not claim

No school fee is claimed in the total, because the district notice does not publish an ADU assessment rule and the elementary feeder varies by address. No traffic or park fee is claimed, because both need a primary-dwelling size to scale. No SMIP charge is claimed, because the FY 2026-27 schedule contains no SMIP line. No trade, sprinkler, or utility figure is claimed, because all of them are unit- or project-specific. No pre-approved plan saving is claimed, because no city catalog or price could be located. No 2,000 sq ft home total is claimed. The $3,301.21 city total is claimed for an 800 sq ft Type VB unit on the February 2026 ICC table, with the Type VA sensitivity stated alongside it. Readers who want the background on plan programs generally should read [California's free ADU plans](/posts/free-adu-plans-california/) and then confirm availability and price with the city before assuming any saving.
