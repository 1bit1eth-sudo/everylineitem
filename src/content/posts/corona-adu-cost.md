Corona is the cheapest ledger in this series, and the reason is a blank column. The DIF Update 2026 (effective July 1, 2026) leaves the ADU column blank for every city impact category — street, signal, drainage, law enforcement, fire, library, Quimby park, aquatic, public meeting, landscape, community facilities. The regional Transportation Uniform Mitigation Fee is $0 because Riverside County Ordinance 824.20 exempts ADUs and JADUs from the program entirely. The Quimby park fee is $0 because it is collected at subdivision map, at $12,708 per single-family unit. Every impact fee is **$0**. Our six-phase total lands at **$305,000, or $381 per square foot** — but it is a floor, not a finished figure. The school district fee and the building permit fee are not yet verified, so they are not in the total at all.

The only fee line in the whole ledger is the sewer capacity charge: **$3,096** for a Secondary Unit at 200 gallons per day × $15.48 per GPD. Planning is $0 — ADUs are processed ministerially, via building plan check. The MSHCP proportional fee is the one impact line that does apply, but it needs the primary dwelling's size to compute, so it is excluded rather than guessed at. The city's preapproved-plan process exists on paper — authorized plans "will be posted when available" — but none have been posted yet. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Corona does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## A blank column, three ways

The $0 impact fee is not one decision but three, and each one is worth naming because each could change independently. The city DIF is blank by design: the DIF Update 2026, effective July 1, 2026, leaves the ADU column empty for every city category, from street and signal to library and community facilities. The regional TUMF is $0 by ordinance: Riverside County Ordinance 824.20 exempts ADUs and JADUs from the Transportation Uniform Mitigation Fee program entirely, not just in Corona but program-wide. And Quimby is $0 by timing: it is a subdivision exaction collected at map, $12,708 per single-family unit, so a backyard ADU on an existing lot never triggers it.

The one impact line that does apply is the MSHCP proportional fee — the Western Riverside County Multiple Species Habitat Conservation Plan fee — and it is the one line this ledger refuses to price. It is proportional, which means it needs the primary dwelling's size, and without that size any number would be a guess. It is excluded, named here, and flagged for the lot-specific calculation a homeowner will need to do with the city.

## Two gaps that matter

This total is a floor, and the two gaps are the reason. We have no verified school district fee for Corona — Corona-Norco Unified's rate is not in our data, and at the $5.38 state maximum it would add $4,304. We have no verified building permit or plan-check fee either — no rate table, no flat fee, nothing to price. Both are real costs a homeowner will pay; neither is in the $305,000. When they are verified, this post will be updated and the total will rise.

The $305,000 also carries one soft footnote. The sewer capacity charge comes from a handout that carries a 2024 seal, and confirmation that the Secondary Unit category is the computation the city currently applies to an ADU is due on December 1. The $3,096 is verified from the handout's own arithmetic — 200 gpd × $15.48 per GPD — but the category confirmation is still open, and it is marked here rather than assumed.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Corona's impact fees are already $0, so SB 1117 changes nothing we can compute here — there is nothing to reduce. See the full comparison at [Corona: 2026 vs 2027, line by line](/sb-1117/corona/). School fees, permit fees and the sewer capacity charge are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and do not change.
