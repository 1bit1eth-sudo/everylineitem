Rosemead's $302,114 is the thinnest total in this batch — and that thinness is the point. One $114 planning line sits on top of the series construction baseline, while the three lines that usually dominate a fee stack — impact fee, school fee, permit — are all blank for documented reasons. Treat this post as a starting line, not a budget.

The one verified line is the **Accessory Dwelling Unit Covenant fee of $114**, from the City of Rosemead FY 2026-27 Comprehensive Fee Schedule. The prior year's schedule listed $111, so the fee is current and moving slowly. It is the only ADU-specific dollar figure in the schedule.

The impact fee is the most consequential blank. The same schedule publishes residential development impact fees of **$6,500 per single-family dwelling unit** in single-use zones and **$6,388** in mixed-use zones, distributed across traffic, public safety, general government, and parks under the Impact Fee Study approved June 9, 2015, and adjusted each fiscal year by the Engineering News Record Los Angeles County construction index under Resolution 2015-07, Section 3. What the schedule does not say is how those per-unit fees apply to an ADU. Charging the full $6,500 on an 800 sq ft ADU would add more than 2% to this total; charging a prorated share would add less; charging nothing would add nothing — and no city document found in this round picks among those. So the DIF is shown, not counted.

The school fee cannot be reduced to one number at all. Rosemead parcels fall in Garvey or Rosemead elementary districts, overlapping Alhambra Unified or El Monte Union high school districts, with small pieces in San Gabriel Unified and Montebello Unified. Until the parcel's district is known, there is no rate to multiply.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the only counted city fee is the $114 covenant, a planning line, not an impact fee.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The DIF's ADU treatment.** The $6,500 / $6,388 per-unit figures are published; their application to an ADU is not. This single determination is worth thousands in either direction and is the first thing to confirm with the city.
2. **The parcel's school district.** Garvey, Rosemead, Alhambra, El Monte Union, San Gabriel, or Montebello — each with its own rate. A single-district determination unlocks the school line.
3. **The building permit.** LA County Title 26 fees plus 50% is a formula, not a figure. Running the county's valuation table for an 800 sq ft ADU would convert it into a countable line.
