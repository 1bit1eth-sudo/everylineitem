25% bigger, but not 25% more expensive. The difference is fees.

On the same LA County site where our [800 sq ft benchmark](/posts/800-sq-ft-adu-cost-california/) costs $320,000, the 1,000 sq ft version lands around **$390,000–$420,000** (table total $392,000). Area grows 25%; the total grows only about 22–28%. The kitchen and bathroom are fixed costs — they don't scale with square footage.

This post is not a size comparison. It's about what happens when you cross 750 sq ft.

## The 750 Sq Ft Cliff

The moment an ADU exceeds 750 sq ft of interior living area, impact fees switch on. Under the proportionality rule in Gov. Code §66311.5, a 1,000 sq ft ADU next to a 2,000 sq ft primary home is assessed at **50% of the primary home's impact fees** (our arithmetic: 1,000/2,000) — the dollar amount still depends entirely on the city's own schedule, so verify it before finalizing plans. See [the 750 sq ft cliff, explained](/posts/750-sq-ft-cliff-explained/).

School fees work differently. Above 500 sq ft, they apply to the **full area** — not just the excess. At the 2026 Level I cap of $5.38/sq ft, a 1,000 sq ft ADU faces up to **$5,380** in school fees.

**SB 1117 takes effect January 1, 2027.** For up to two ADUs per lot, only the square footage **above** 750 sq ft counts toward impact-fee calculations. At this size, that's 250 sq ft. If you're planning now, that date matters.

|---|---|---|

| Framing | $48,000 | $58,000 |
| Envelope | $42,000 | $50,000 |
| Systems + interior | $147,000 | $178,000 |
| Landscape | $13,000 | $16,000 |
| **Total** | **$320,000** | **$392,000** |
| **Per sq ft** | **$400** | **$392** |

Note the per-square-foot figure **drops** at the larger size. That's the fixed-cost cliff working in reverse: the kitchen, bathroom, and utility connections cost roughly the same whether the unit is 800 or 1,000 sq ft.

## What to Watch

- **Impact fees** are the single biggest swing item at this size. Verify your city's exact schedule before finalizing plans.
- **School fees** at $5.38/sq ft are capped statewide, but the district's adopted rate may be lower — check.
- **SB 1117** (effective 2027-01-01) only helps if your permit is issued after that date. A December permit locks in the old math.
