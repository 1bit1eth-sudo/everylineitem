Unincorporated San Mateo County's $314,637 is the richest floor in this batch — and the first county draft in the series. Unlike a city post, it has a genuine permit stack computed from the county's own schedules, not a single fee line. It also has the batch's most honest complication: the school fee depends on the parcel's district, and the surcharges are quantified but deliberately uncounted.

The permit stack is the core of the post. The Building Service Fee Schedule (effective April 1, 2026) charges new residential construction at **$8.33 per square foot**, covering permit processing, plan review, and inspection — **$6,664** on 800 sq ft. The Full Review application filing fee adds **$595**. The Planning Service Fee Schedule (effective July 23, 2026) charges **$887** for Planning Review of Building Permit Plans including one resubmittal. The county fire review adds **$187** for single-family new construction under Note 7. That is **$8,333** in flat county fees before the school line.

The school line is **$4,304**, but it must be read with its qualifier: it is the Menlo Park City School District plus Sequoia Union High School District combined rate at the $5.38 statutory limit, adopted by MPCSD Resolution 25.26.15 (April 16, 2026, effective June 15, 2026) with a 40% / 60% high-school / feeder split. Unincorporated county parcels fall in many districts; a North Fair Oaks parcel, for example, is in different districts entirely. The $4,304 is counted because it is a verified 2026 adoption for a real subset of county parcels — and the draft says so, rather than pretending it is countywide.

Two confirmed zeros anchor the stack: the Affordable Housing Impact Fee **exempts second units** on the county's own fees page, and the Zoning Ordinance (§8.392.050(16)) provides ADUs **shall not be subject to design review** — both $0, not missing.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the Affordable Housing Impact Fee is already $0 for second units, no per-square-foot impact fee is counted, and the school fee is untouched by SB 1117.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The surcharges.** $1,621.96 is quantified from the schedules but excluded on a base question. Confirming the county's combined-fee worksheet at permit time would move the floor to about $316,259 — the single largest known adjustment.
2. **The parcel's school district.** The counted $4,304 is the Menlo Park-area pair. Parcels in other districts pay those districts' rates, which may be lower or at the same $5.38 limit. District determination is mandatory before budgeting this line.
3. **Location-specific fees.** A Mid-Coast parcel owes park fees; a parcel needing a geotechnical report owes $892 and possibly $773 more; roadway mitigation applies countywide at an unlocated rate. Any of these can add four figures to a specific parcel's total.

## How San Mateo County compares

Only published posts are used here. Published Bay Area totals: Palo Alto **~$303,000**, Fremont **$314,000**, Campbell **~$318,000**, Oakland **$322,000**. The county's $314,637 sits mid-band — and unlike the city floors around it, it already counts a full permit stack. Add the $1,621.96 in surcharges and it reaches about $316,259, still mid-band, before any parcel-specific fees. That completeness is the point: this floor is closer to a budget than the thinner city floors in this batch.

## What this post does not claim

It does not claim the school fee is $4,304 for every unincorporated parcel — it is the Menlo Park-area pair, and other districts differ. It does not claim surcharges are $0 — it claims $1,621.96 is quantified but excluded on a worksheet question. It does not claim sewer and water cost $0 — they are served by special districts and entirely uncounted. Construction phases 2–6 are the series baseline, not county bids.
