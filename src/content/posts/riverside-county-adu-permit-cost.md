Riverside County is the rare California jurisdiction where the fee story runs in reverse: the county's own impact fees are **$0 for ADUs by exemption**, and the number you actually have to budget for at the permit counter is a **$6,472.45 deposit** — which is not the final cost.

Read the scope line first, because it decides whether this post applies to you. This post covers **unincorporated Riverside County only** — the areas where the county's Transportation & Land Management Agency (TLMA) is the permitting authority. If your parcel sits inside an incorporated city (Riverside, Moreno Valley, Corona, Temecula, Murrieta, Hemet, Perris, Menifee, Lake Elsinore, Palm Springs, and the rest), that city has its own fee schedule and its own rules. For the city of Riverside specifically, see [Riverside ADU Cost](/posts/riverside-adu-cost/) — a different post, a different permit, a $1,006 city permit bill.

## The short answer

| 800 sq ft detached ADU · Unincorporated Riverside County · 2026 | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Building permit (BRS-ADU) | **$6,472.45 deposit** — final cost calculated at application |
| County development impact fee (Ord. 659) | **$0 — ADUs exempt** |
| Western-county transportation fee (TUMF, Ord. 824) | **$0 — ADUs exempt** |
| School fee, Hemet USD (confirmed) | **$4,304** (800 × $5.38) |
| School fee, Corona-Norco USD (unconfirmed currency) | $2,784 (800 × $3.48) |
| Water / sewer connection, typical (EMWD) | **$0** (existing service) |
| Free pre-approved plans | **4 designs, free PDF** |
| 750 sq ft cliff | **Moot for county fees** — both already exempt |

The county's fee schedule and its free-plan list were re-checked on 5 Nov 2026: the $6,472.45 deposit and all four plan sizes are still current.

## The $6,472.45 deposit, explained

The TLMA Building & Safety fee schedule lists two ADU permit types at the same deposit: Additional Dwelling Unit (BRS-ADU) and Permit Ready Accessory Dwelling Unit (BRS-PRADU), each **$6,472.45** — identical to the deposit for a new single-family detached dwelling. The county treats an ADU permit like a new dwelling for deposit purposes.

Three things about that number matter more than the number itself:

1. **It is a deposit, not a price.** The schedule's own basis is "Base Fee – Final Permit cost will be calculated at the time of permit application." Actual charges are drawn from the deposit and may be less. Do not budget $6,472.45 as the final permit cost, and do not compare it directly to other cities' flat permit fees.
2. **Surcharges sit on top.** SMI, processing, microfilm, LMS, and green building fees apply in addition to the deposit draw.
3. **The trades are separate.** Electrical, plumbing, and mechanical sub-permits are separate fixed-fee permits, not included in the BRS-ADU deposit.

There is no separate county "ADU application" planning fee on the schedule — unlike some cities' $2,000-plus planning ADU fees, the county's ADU cost at the counter is the deposit plus its draw.

## Why county impact fees are $0

Two separate exemptions, both in the ordinance text:

**County development impact fee (Ordinance No. 659).** Section 17 (Exemptions) expressly exempts detached second units and attached second units — "second units" is the county zoning code's term for ADUs. The ordinance's findings state the Board "refrains from imposing Fees on such development at this time." An 800 sq ft detached ADU in unincorporated county pays **$0** under Ord. 659 at any size.

**Western-county transportation fee (TUMF, Ordinance No. 824, WRCOG).** Section 824.20 states that ADUs and JADUs, as defined by state law, "are exempt from TUMF and shall not be counted in determining residential land use classification." The WRCOG Planning Directors Committee confirmed in February 2025 that ADUs are exempt with no anticipated changes. Western-county ADU TUMF: **$0**.

That is why the 750 sq ft cliff — the rule that normally decides whether an ADU pays impact fees — is moot for county fees here. Both fees are already $0 before the cliff ever applies.

## The eastern-county exception

The Coachella Valley (eastern county) transportation fee, TUMF under Ordinance No. 673 (CVAG), is different: no ADU exemption was found in its text. State law therefore governs — no impact fee under 750 sq ft, proportionate charging at 750 sq ft and above. The published single-family detached rate is **$2,740 per dwelling unit** (January 2024, with annual CPI adjustments since — the 2026 rate is not separately confirmed). On an 800 sq ft ADU with a 2,000 sq ft primary home, the proportionate 2026 figure is roughly **$1,096** (800 ÷ 2,000 × $2,740) — an example, not a county-published ADU figure.

One more eastern-county item is genuinely unknown, and it is the largest open dollar figure in this post: the MSHCP Local Development Mitigation Fee (Ordinance No. 810) runs **$4,632 per dwelling unit** for residential under 8 units per acre (FY2027 schedule), but whether the county assesses it on ADUs — in full, proportionately, or not at all — was not documented in county sources. Until that is confirmed, any eastern-county ADU total that includes or excludes $4,632 is a guess. It is flagged for the January 2027 re-verification, not estimated here.

## School fees: $4,304 confirmed, the rest district-by-district

Unincorporated Riverside County has no single school fee because it is served by more than 20 school districts. Two are documented:

- **Hemet Unified — $5.38 per sq ft, confirmed.** Resolution No. 4090 (adopted March 3, 2026) adopts the 2026 Fee Justification Study at the SAB maximum: $5.38 residential. On 800 sq ft: **$4,304**. Hemet USD serves the majority of Hemet plus large unincorporated territory (Winchester, Homeland, Sage, Aguanga, Anza, Idyllwild, East Hemet).
- **Corona-Norco Unified — $3.48 per sq ft, currency unconfirmed.** The district's official developer-fee page posts Level 1 new residential at $3.48 ($2,784 on 800 sq ft), but the page figure was retrieved from a cached copy and sits well below the 2026 SAB maximum, so it may predate the 2026 adjustment. Treat $2,784 as provisional.

The state 500 sq ft rule applies regardless of district: ADUs of 500 sq ft or less pay $0 in school fees; an 800 sq ft ADU pays the full district rate. School fees are not impact fees for SB 1117 purposes and do not change in 2027.

## Water, sewer, and septic: $0 typical, with two caveats

The county's unincorporated areas are served by many water providers; the largest in the western county is Eastern Municipal Water District (EMWD). EMWD's 2026 charges include a water Financial Participation Charge of **$7,738 per Equivalent Meter Size**, a sewer Financial Participation Charge of **$3,626 per EDU**, and a sewer Treatment Plant Capacity Charge of **$6,876 per EDU**.

The typical ADU — tying into the existing lateral and the existing meter — creates no new service, so the typical additional connection/capacity charge is **$0**. Two caveats: EMWD's ADU-specific assessment practice (whether a new EDU is assessed for an ADU on existing sewer service) was not found in its published fee documents, and a separate new water meter, if required, would trigger the participation charges above (a 1-inch meter at EMS 2.0 would be $15,476 in water participation charges alone, before installation). Separately, large rural unincorporated areas are on septic, and the county Department of Environmental Health's septic evaluation and permit fees for ADUs were not researched for this post.

## Free pre-approved plans: four designs, county permits only

The TLMA Permit Ready ADU program publishes four free plan sets:

| Plan | Size | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan #1 | 499 sq ft | Studio, single story |
| Plan #2 | 625 sq ft | 1 bed / 1 bath, single story |
| Plan #3 | 749 sq ft | 2 bed / 1 bath, single story |
| Plan #4 | 909 sq ft | 2 bed / 2 bath, single story |

Traditional, Craftsman, and Spanish exterior elevations. Eligibility covers SFR-zoned lots developed or proposed with a single-family dwelling, excluding flood-hazard and environmental-constraint areas. The program applies to unincorporated-county permits only. Note the benchmark gap: the standard 800 sq ft example sits between Plan #3 (749) and Plan #4 (909) — there is no free 800 sq ft county plan.

## SB 1117: almost nothing moves

Effective January 1, 2027, SB 1117 (Chapter 809) limits covered impact fees on properties with two or fewer ADUs to the area above 750 sq ft. In unincorporated Riverside County, almost nothing moves, because the fees are already $0 or excluded:

| Line item (800 sq ft ADU) | 2026 | 2027-01-01 (SB 1117) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| County DIF (Ord. 659) | $0 (exempt) | $0 — no change |
| Western TUMF (Ord. 824) | $0 (exempt) | $0 — no change |
| Eastern TUMF (Ord. 673, CVAG only) | ≈ $1,096 proportionate | ≈ $68.50 (50 sq ft basis) — **≈ −94%** |
| School fees | $4,304 / $2,784 | No change — school fees are not covered impact fees |
| Building permit deposit | $6,472.45 | No change — a fee-schedule fee, not an impact fee |
| Water / sewer connection | $0 typical | No change — connection/capacity charges are excluded |

The only live SB 1117 wire in the county is the eastern TUMF.
