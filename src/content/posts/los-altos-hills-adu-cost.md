Los Altos Hills waives the fees other towns charge. **Pathway Development Impact Fee: $0. Recreation In-Lieu Fee: $0. Storm Drainage Fee: $0.** All three are printed at zero for an ADU on the town's 2026 Impact Fee Schedule — not missing, not unconfirmed, zero. The school fee is the state maximum $5.38 per square foot from two districts combined, $4,304. The permit column is $1,555: plan review $725 and compliance inspections $830. Our six-phase total lands at **$308,000, or $385 per square foot**.

There is one more fee before the counter: a $410 pre-application meeting, the town's only planning charge for an ADU. Water and sewer have no verified capacity line on file — an ADU on an existing lot shares the existing service under state law. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Los Altos Hills does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## Three impact fees, all $0

The 2026 Impact Fee Schedule lists three fees that could apply to an ADU, and prices all three at zero. The Pathway Development Impact Fee: $0. The Recreation In-Lieu Fee: $0. The Storm Drainage Fee: $0. In most cities in this series, at least one of these lines carries a real number — Redlands charges $0.5113 per square foot for storm drainage alone, and Upland's storm drain DIF is $0.90 per square foot. Los Altos Hills charges nothing on any of them.

The practical effect is that the 750 sq ft cliff does not matter here. State law (Gov. Code §66311.5) already zeroes impact fees at 750 sq ft or less, and SB 1117 will reduce them above 750 from 2027 — but there is nothing to reduce. An 800 sq ft ADU pays $0 in impact fees in 2026 and $0 in 2027. The town has already done, by schedule, what the state law does by statute.

## Two districts, one rate

The school fee is the one line in this post that needs two sources to explain. Los Altos Hills sits across two school districts — Los Altos School District (elementary) and Mountain View-Los Altos Union High School District (secondary) — and the developer fee is a combined Level I rate of **$5.38 per square foot**, the state maximum, from the districts' joint 2026 developer fee study. On 800 sq ft that is **$4,304**.

The fee applies from 500 sq ft, so the full 800 sq ft is levied — there is no partial-area math at our size. The 500 sq ft cliff (SB 543) means an ADU under 500 sq ft would owe $0 in school fees; at 800 sq ft the whole area pays. It is the only fee in this post that reaches four figures, and it is more than double the entire permit column.

## A $410 conversation before you build

Before the permit column starts, the town charges $410 for an ADU pre-application meeting. It is the only planning charge for an ADU in the town's materials, and it is the smallest line in the post that is not $0. The meeting is where the town tells a homeowner what the lot can hold — setbacks, height, access — before the plan review clock starts. The plan review itself is $725 and the compliance inspections are $830, so the full permit-side total is $1,965 including the meeting, or $1,555 without it.

Water and sewer have no verified capacity line on file. An ADU on an existing lot shares the existing service under state law (Gov. Code §66311.5), which bars the town from requiring a new or separate connection — so the example pays $0 on those lines, but that $0 is the law's, not a priced line in the town's schedule.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Los Altos Hills has three impact-fee lines and all three are $0, so SB 1117 changes nothing we can compute here — there is nothing to reduce. See the full comparison at [Los Altos Hills: 2026 vs 2027, line by line](/sb-1117/los-altos-hills/). School fees, permit fees and water/sewer charges are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and do not change.
