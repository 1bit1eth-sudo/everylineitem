The one verified line is the **Property Development Tax of $300 per dwelling unit**, charged at building permit application under Walnut Municipal Code Chapter 7.16. One detached ADU is one dwelling unit, so the line is $300.

The school fee is the most consequential blank. Walnut Valley Unified School District commissioned a new developer fee justification study in August 2025 (Jack Schreder & Associates, board agenda item), but the resulting current rate was not found in this round, and the last published study figure located ($4.08 per square foot) is years old and predates two SAB cycles. Using it would understate the line by an unknown amount, so it is shown, not counted. A current district posting would unlock the school line immediately.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the only counted city line is the $300 tax, not an impact fee.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The Walnut Valley Unified rate.** A single current district posting unlocks the school line, likely the largest missing fee. The August 2025 study commission suggests a current rate exists somewhere; it was not found in this round.
2. **The city's fee schedule.** Walnut's current master fee schedule would convert the permit, planning, and any impact lines from blanks into figures.
3. **Sewer and water connections.** No verified rate was found. The serving utilities' capacity and connection charges are entirely uncounted.
