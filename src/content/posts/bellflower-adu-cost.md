**DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.** Numbers below are verified against City of Bellflower and Bellflower Unified documents as of October 26, 2026, unless marked otherwise.

Bellflower ADU fees come with a word no other city in this batch uses: "Varies." The city's development-fees guide publishes exact per-square-foot rates for single-family homes — and then, for ADUs, declines to name a number at all. An 800 sq ft detached ADU here lands at **$302,000, or $378 per square foot** — a floor, not a total. It counts the series-baseline construction phases and $14,000 of design. The planning fee is $0. Everything else — the permit, the impact fees, the school fee, the sewer charges — is excluded, each for a stated reason, and each marked "verify with city" or "verify with district" below.

One line is deliberately absent. A verified floor total for a 2,000 sq ft single-family new construction cannot be stated from official sources: fee schedules price permits and fees, not construction, so no such figure is verified here and none is estimated. The 2,000 sq ft figure matters only as the proportionality denominator — an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% of the primary's size, the fraction the city's guide uses for ADUs over 750 sq ft.

> **The ADU impact-fee box.** California law bars any impact fee on an ADU under 750 square feet, and requires proportional charges at 750 square feet or more (Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A)). Bellflower's own documents apply the same rule twice: the ADU handout states that development fees are applicable for ADUs over 750 sq ft, and the development-fees guide states the proportional footnote. For an 800 sq ft ADU, the fraction against a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% — but the guide's single-family rates are per-square-foot inputs and the ADU line itself reads "Varies," so the city has not published an ADU dollar amount to apply the fraction to. The temptation to add the four single-family rates ($25.36 per sq ft) and multiply by 800 sq ft would produce a full single-family figure, not an ADU figure, and it is not used here. School fees sit outside this box: they apply from 500 sq ft under the handout, a different threshold from the 750 sq ft impact-fee line.

## Three things that can still move the number

**1. The unopened permit schedule.** Resolution No. 26-32 is the most consequential unverified document in this batch: a brand-new building-permit fee schedule, effective October 9, 2026, whose amounts could not be opened. Every permit-related line in this post — including the 12% and 4% planning surcharges, which are percentages of the permit — waits on that document. Until its contents are verified, the permit line is not merely unknown, it is unknowable from public text found here.

**2. The "Varies" footnote's missing input.** The guide's proportional rule is precise about the method and silent about the inputs: it needs the primary dwelling's square footage and the city's ADU calculation, neither of which a guide can supply. Two identical 800 sq ft ADUs behind different-sized houses would pay different impact fees under this rule. That is why no illustration is offered: any illustration would have to invent a primary dwelling.

**3. The school-fee rate and the district map.** The handout puts an 800 sq ft ADU squarely in school-fee territory (over 500 sq ft), but the rate is missing and the district is not certain: the city's own news page points permit applicants to Bellflower Unified, while noting the certificate-of-compliance process, yet parts of the city may fall in other districts. Both the rate and the serving district need confirmation before any school-fee dollar figure can be stated.
