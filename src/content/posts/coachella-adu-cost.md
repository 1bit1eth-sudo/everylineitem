Coachella's $305,832 is the thinnest total in this batch — and that thinness is the point. One fee line, verified to a school district document, sits on top of the series construction baseline. Everything the city itself might charge is still a blank, because the city's own fee tables did not surface in this round of research. Treat this post as a starting line, not a budget.

The one verified line is the school fee. Coachella Valley Unified School District (CVUSD) charges **$4.79 per square foot** for residential construction, adopted November 17, 2022 and effective January 16, 2023, per the district's fee justification report and its fee-increase notice. On 800 square feet that is **$3,832**. It is also possibly stale: the state maximum has since risen to $5.38 per square foot, and whether CVUSD adopted a higher rate in 2024 or 2026 is unconfirmed. If the district is now at the maximum, the school line would be $4,304 and this total would rise by $472.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The city impact fee schedule.** The single biggest unknown. Coachella's annual DIF reports exist; the rate table behind them was not extracted this round. Once it is, the impact line — and the SB 1117 proration on it — can be computed.
2. **The building permit.** No official city permit table was located. Valuation-based or flat, it will add thousands to the floor.
3. **The CVUSD rate's currency.** $4.79 is a 2022 adoption. A 2024 or 2026 district adoption at the state maximum would add up to $472 to the school line alone.
