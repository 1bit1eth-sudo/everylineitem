Burlingame's ledger has a hole in the middle of it, and the hole is a single ambiguous phrase. Water capacity is **$3.04 per square foot** and sewer capacity is **$4.63 per square foot**, both charged "over 150 sq ft" — but the fee schedule does not settle whether the assessable basis is the 650 sq ft above 150 or the full 800 sq ft. Water is $1,976 on the first reading and $2,432 on the second; sewer is $3,009.50 and $3,704. That one ambiguity moves the total by $1,150.50, so the ledger is a range: **$309,000–$310,000, or $386–$388 per square foot** — and it is a floor, not a finished figure. The school district fee and the general building permit fee are not yet verified, so they are not in the total at all.

What is verified is itemised and ordinary. The planning application fee is $950. Plumbing, mechanical and electrical permits are $729 — three permits at $0.18 per square foot plus a $99 issuance fee each. The Affordable Housing Impact Fee is $0 for a single detached ADU. The 0.2% General Plan maintenance fee is a verified rate, but it is a percentage of building valuation and no valuation basis is on file, so it is not counted. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Burlingame does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## The $1,150.50 question

The capacity charges are the whole story of this ledger, and they turn on four words: "over 150 sq ft". The schedule prices water capacity at $3.04 per square foot and sewer capacity at $4.63 per square foot for a detached ADU over 150 sq ft. On the natural reading, only the area above 150 sq ft is assessed — 650 sq ft — giving water $1,976 and sewer $3,009.50. On the other reading, the full 800 sq ft is assessed, giving water $2,432 and sewer $3,704. The difference is $456 on water and $694.50 on sewer: $1,150.50 in total, which is exactly the width of the range in this post.

Both readings are recorded as confirmed figures because the rate itself is confirmed and the schedule does not fix the basis. The basis is due to be fixed on December 1, when this post will be updated to a single figure. Until then, the honest total is a range — and a homeowner budgeting at the low end should know the high end is $1,150.50 away.

## Two gaps that matter

This total is a floor, and the two gaps are the reason. We have no verified school district fee for Burlingame: the city sits across two school districts, and neither rate is in our data. At the $5.38 state maximum, a school fee would add $4,304. We have no verified general building permit fee either — only the three trade permits at $729 are priced, and the 0.2% General Plan maintenance fee cannot be computed without a valuation basis. The ADU submittal packet shows a $1,091 example total ($950 application fee plus a $141 Categorical Exemption), which hints at the shape of the permit column, but a packet example is not a fee schedule line, so the $141 stays out of the total. Both gaps are real costs a homeowner will pay; neither is in the $309,000–$310,000. When they are verified, this post will be updated and the total will rise.
