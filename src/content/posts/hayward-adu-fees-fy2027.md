> **Published:** 12 Dec 2026 · **Last verified:** 30 Oct 2026 · **Next review:** Jan 2027

Hayward's Adopted Master Fee Schedule FY2026-27, effective July 1, 2026, moves ADU fees in both directions: the park impact fee falls, the sewer connection charge rises, and a small review fee is now documented for ADUs. All three lines below were read in the official schedule on October 30, 2026.

## The three FY2027 lines

**Park impact fee: $3,379 (was $3,812).** The schedule's Park Impact Fees – Residential row charges ADUs of 750+ sq ft **$3,379**. ADUs of 750 sq ft or less are exempt under Gov. Code §66311.5. The whole park table moved down in FY2026-27 — the studio/0-bedroom rate is $3,789, one-bedroom $5,931, two-bedroom $10,699, three-bedroom $18,685, four-or-more $25,991.

**Housing review fee: $158 for ADUs.** The schedule's Housing Review Fee row charges buildings with 1–4 dwelling units $474, buildings with 5 or more units $790, and **ADUs $158**. Unlike the park fee, the schedule states no size qualifier for this row.

**Sewer connection charge: $5,825 (was $5,295).** The sanitary sewer table lists ADU connections at $5,295.00 effective September 1, 2025, rising to **$5,825.00 effective September 1, 2026**. For comparison, a single-family connection is $14,559 on the same date. This is a connection charge, not a monthly service rate.

## The 800 sq ft snapshot

| Line | Amount | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Park impact fee | $3,379 | ADU 750+ sq ft, FY2027 |
| Housing review fee | $158 | ADU flat, FY2027 |
| Sewer connection | $5,825 | ADU per unit, Sep 1, 2026 |
| School developer fee | $4,304 | 800 sq ft × $5.38 (Hayward Unified, existing verification) |
| **Total** | **$13,666** | |

Two things about this total. First, it mixes vintages deliberately and labels them: park, housing review, and sewer are FY2027-verified; the school rate is our existing verification for Hayward Unified, not re-verified in the FY2027 documents. Second, it is a fee snapshot, not a permit total — plan check, inspection, and other permit fees are separate and not counted here.

## What SB 1117 does and does not change

SB 1117 (Chapter 809, Statutes of 2026) narrows impact fees for a lot's first two ADUs to the square footage above 750, starting January 1, 2027. For Hayward, that rule has no published application to the park fee: the fee is flat, not per-square-foot, and the city has not published a conversion to a 50-sq-ft basis. We therefore claim no 2027 park figure. School developer fees are levied under a separate statute (Ed. Code §17620) and are unchanged by SB 1117. Sewer connection and capacity charges are excluded from SB 1117's proration. The lines that carry into 2027 unchanged on today's documents are the $158 review fee, the $5,825 sewer connection, and the $5.38 school rate.
