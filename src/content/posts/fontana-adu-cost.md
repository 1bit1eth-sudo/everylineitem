Fontana's development impact fees total **$20,563 per new single-family home** — park development alone is $7,733, circulation mitigation another $6,685. It is one of the heaviest DIF loads in this series. And then the city published a free ADU plan at **738 square feet** — twelve feet under the 750 sq ft impact-fee cliff — that pays exactly $0 of it.

That tension is the story of building an ADU in Fontana: a fee structure that punishes size, and a city plan set engineered to dodge it. Our 800 sq ft benchmark, priced phase by phase, lands at **about $319,000, or $399 per square foot** — a dollar under the LA County baseline, built from very different parts than its neighbors.

## The 738 sq ft loophole

Fontana's Standard ADU Plans program publishes three detached designs. Read them as a strategy document:

- **Morning Glory** — 462 sq ft, 1 bed/1 bath: under both the 500 school-fee cliff and the 750 impact-fee cliff.
- **Carnation** — 738 sq ft, 2 bed/2 bath: under the 750 cliff by 12 feet. Pays $0 in impact fees.
- **Sunflower** — 1,143 sq ft, 3 bed/2 bath: over the cliff, full pro-rata DIF.

The Carnation is the telling one. A 2-bedroom, 2-bath ADU — genuinely livable — drawn twelve feet under the line where $10,967 in fees switch on. The city is not subtle about it, and neither should you be: if your program fits in 738 sq ft, Fontana's own plan set hands you a five-figure discount.

Fontana lands a dollar under the baseline almost by accident: a transparent flat permit ($3,195 — no valuation math, no surprises) and a school fee at the statutory maximum roughly cancel out, leaving the DIF as the swing variable. On a large primary dwelling the pro-rata shrinks; on a small one it grows. And from January 2027, SB 1117 rewrites the math anyway — fees only on square footage above 750, which turns ~$10,967 into something closer to ~$685 on our example. The 2026 permit timing question is worth real money here.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Fontana's May 2026 DIF justification study already states the current pro-rata rule in plain language; the marginal rule will require a new schedule. We found no mention of SB 1117 in city materials; the January 2027 quarterly review will check. School fees ($4,304) are outside SB 1117 and unchanged.
