**DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.** Numbers below are verified against City of Buena Park and Orange County Sanitation District documents as of October 26, 2026, unless marked otherwise.

Buena Park ADU fees are the one file in this batch where a regional agency, not the city, supplies the only hard number. The city's own building-permit schedule could not be located, its adopted impact-fee schedule could not be verified, and its ADU code names only one fee category for ADUs — parks — without a dollar amount. An 800 sq ft detached ADU here lands at **$304,000, or $380 per square foot** — a floor, not a total. It counts the series-baseline construction phases, $14,000 of design, and a $1,710 sewer capacity charge verified from the sanitation district's own table. The building permit, the park fees, and the school fee are excluded, each for a stated reason, and each marked "verify with city" or "verify with district" below.

One line is deliberately absent. A verified floor total for a 2,000 sq ft single-family new construction cannot be stated from official sources: fee schedules price permits and fees, not construction, so no such figure is verified here and none is estimated. The 2,000 sq ft figure matters only as the proportionality denominator — an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% of the primary's size, the fraction the city's ADU code and the state rule use for fees at 750 sq ft and above.

> **The ADU impact-fee box.** California law bars any impact fee on an ADU under 750 square feet, and requires proportional charges at 750 square feet or more (Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A)). Buena Park's ADU code applies the same line and names only one fee category for ADUs: park land dedications and in-lieu fees, proportionate for ADUs of 750 square feet or more. For an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft house, the fraction is 40% — but the adopted schedule's park amounts are unverified, so no dollar amount follows. The 2024 nexus study's parks figure ($10.47 per sq ft) is a maximum-justified figure, not an adopted fee, and using it in the formula would dress an unadopted ceiling as a bill. It stays a reference line. Note the boundary: the sewer capacity charge in this post's total is not an "impact fee" under the statute — it is a capacity charge stated for ADUs on the sanitation district's own table, which is why it, unlike the park fees, can be counted.

## Three things that can still move the number

**1. The missing permit schedule.** No current Buena Park building-permit schedule was located — only old planning-fee schedules and a 2022 public-works schedule, neither of which prices a 2026 residential permit. The permit is normally one of the largest soft-cost lines, so its absence is the single biggest gap in this floor. The city should be asked for the current schedule before any budget uses this post.

**2. The adopted park-fee amounts.** The ADU code points to an adopted Development Impact Fee Schedule that this session could not verify — only the 2024 nexus study's maximum-justified figures, which the study itself labels as maxima. Council action adopting the study and a new fee chapter was reported by a third-party summary, but the year and the adopted amounts were not verified against a city resolution. Until the adopted schedule is in hand, the park line cannot be priced even proportionally.

**3. The school-district map.** Buena Park is not a single-district city: elementary and high school service varies by parcel, and no adopted rate for Buena Park parcels was verified. Unlike Arcadia, where one district's notice supplies a rate, Buena Park's school fee needs two lookups (the parcel's elementary district and its high school district) before any dollar figure can be stated.
