> **Published:** 10 Dec 2026 · **Last verified:** 30 Oct 2026 · **Next review:** Jan 2027

On May 20, 2026, the Encinitas City Council adopted Resolution 2026-34, approving a new Citywide Development Impact Fee Nexus Study and amending the city's development impact fees. The new fees took effect **July 20, 2026**. For ADUs, the change is large: the old per-unit fees are gone, replaced by per-square-foot rates — and an ADU at or above 750 sq ft now carries a five-figure impact bill.

Here is the adopted table, the worked math for an 800 sq ft ADU, and the one honest caveat: the table below covers the Development Services Department's fees. Other departments assess more, and those figures are not in the city's handout.

## The ADU rule

The nexus study's ADU section states the rule plainly: impact fees will not be charged for an ADU under 750 square feet. For an ADU of 750 square feet or larger, the fee is calculated by multiplying the city's single-family residential impact-fee rate by the ADU's square footage. That is the rule we apply below.

## The adopted Development Services fees

The city's July 2026 Development Impact Fees handout (PLN-239) lists these single-family residential rates, all under Resolution 2026-34, effective July 20, 2026:

| Fee | Single-family rate (per sq ft) |
| --- | --- |
| Park Development | $13.56 |
| Parkland Acquisition | $3.70 |
| Open Space Land Acquisition | $0.78 |
| Recreational Trails | $0.16 |
| Community Facilities | $1.40 |
| **Subtotal** | **$19.60** |
| Administrative fee (5%) | $0.98 |
| **Total** | **$20.58** |

## What an 800 sq ft ADU owes

On the adopted Development Services basis:

- Impact fees: 800 × $19.60 = **$15,680**
- Administrative fee: 5% × $15,680 = **$784**
- **Total: $16,464**

For scale, the study's comparison table puts the old single-family total at $9.13 per sq ft. The new program roughly doubles the per-square-foot burden even before other departments' fees — and the park fee alone jumps from $2.28 to $13.56 adopted ($13.76 in the study's proposal draft).

## The caveat: this is not necessarily the whole bill

The handout covers fees assessed by the Development Services Department. It states explicitly that other departments — Public Works, Fire Prevention, the Building Division — may also assess development fees, and that school district fees and sewer/water fees (Cardiff Sanitation District, Leucadia Wastewater District, San Dieguito Water District, Olivenhain Water District) are separate.

The nexus study's own proposed program total for single-family residential is **$31.68 per sq ft**, including Traffic Mitigation ($5.86), Fire Mitigation ($0.52), Flood Control ($3.96), and the $1.51 administrative fee. Those three departmental fees are study-proposal figures from the March 2026 draft, not adopted figures from the July handout — the adopted handout values differ slightly from the study's proposal even for the five fees it does list (park $13.56 adopted vs $13.76 proposed, for example). So we do not present $31.68 as the adopted bill.

The honest way to read it: **$16,464 is the confirmed floor** for an 800 sq ft ADU (adopted Development Services fees plus admin). If the other departments' adopted rates match the study's proposal, the full-program figure would be 800 × $31.68 = **$25,344**. Treat that second number as a planning upper bound, not a bill, until the other departments' adopted schedules are confirmed.

## What SB 1117 changes on January 1, 2027

SB 1117 (Chapter 809, Statutes of 2026) narrows impact fees for a lot's first two ADUs to only the square footage above 750, starting January 1, 2027. On the adopted Development Services basis, an 800 sq ft ADU's bill falls to 50 × $20.58 = **$1,029**. On the study-proposed full-program basis, it would be 50 × $31.68 = **$1,584**. School fees are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and do not change. Permits pulled in 2026 are assessed under 2026 rules regardless of when construction starts.
