Upland's impact fees are a parks budget with four other lines attached. **Parks DIF: $4.80 per square foot** — 61% of all per-square-foot lines combined, and more than six times the next-largest line (Storm Drain at $0.90). The five DIF lines total $6.98 per square foot, $5,584 on an 800 sq ft ADU. Water adds $0.87 and sewer $0.05 per square foot, $736 together. Our six-phase total lands at **$308,000, or $385 per square foot** — but it is a floor. The school district fee and the building permit and plan-check fees are not yet verified, so they are not in the total at all.

The cliff is published where a homeowner can actually find it: the city's own ADU page, uplandadu.org/permit, lists every line with a 750 sq ft minimum. Build at 750 or under and all five DIF lines are $0. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Upland does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## Parks is 61% of the per-square-foot total

The Parks DIF is the story of this post. At $4.80 per square foot it is 61% of the $7.90 per square foot total of all per-square-foot lines — and 69% of the $6.98 impact-fee lines alone. On 800 sq ft it is $3,840, more than the other four DIF lines combined ($1,744) and more than five times the water and sewer lines together ($736). A homeowner building an ADU in Upland is, in fee terms, mostly buying parkland.

The comparison that makes the point is Redlands, published the same day: eight impact lines totalling $5.4405 per square foot, with the largest single line (Transportation at $1.3954) at 26% of the total. Upland has five lines totalling $6.98, with one line at 61%. The two cities charge similar totals — $4,352 in Redlands, $5,584 in Upland — but the shapes are opposites: Redlands spreads the cost, Upland concentrates it in parks. If the Parks DIF ever moves, Upland's ADU total moves with it; nothing else in the schedule has that leverage.

## The cliff is on the city's own ADU page

Most cities in this series bury the 750 sq ft cliff in an ordinance or a fee schedule PDF. Upland publishes it on uplandadu.org/permit — the city's own ADU page — with every line carrying a 750 sq ft minimum. The DIF lines are General Facilities, Police, Parks, Transportation and Storm Drain; water and sewer carry the same minimum. Build at 750 sq ft or under and all seven lines are $0 under Gov. Code §66311.5. Build at 800 and they total $6,320.

That transparency is worth noting because the cliff is the single most valuable number in the post for a homeowner deciding on size. At 750 sq ft the fee column is $0; at 800 it is $6,320; at 1,000 it is $7,900. The 50 sq ft between 750 and 800 costs $6,320 in fees — $126.40 per square foot of fee for 50 square feet of house. That is the cliff, stated plainly on the city's own page.

## Two gaps that matter

This total is a floor, and the two gaps are the reason. We have no verified school district fee for Upland: the Upland Unified School District rate is not in our data, and at the $5.38 state maximum it would add $4,304. We have no verified building permit or plan-check fee either — no rate table, no flat fee, nothing to price. Both are real costs a homeowner will pay; neither is in the $308,000. When they are verified, this post will be updated and the total will rise.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Upland's five DIF lines are all per-square-foot, so the saving is exactly 750 × $6.98 — **$5,235** at 1,000 sq ft, and the same $5,235 at 800 sq ft, because the exempted first 750 sq ft is identical at any size above the cliff. See the full comparison at [Upland: 2026 vs 2027, line by line](/sb-1117/upland/). School fees, permit fees and water/sewer charges are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and do not change.
