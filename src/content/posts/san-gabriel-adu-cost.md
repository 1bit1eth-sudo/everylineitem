**DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.** Numbers below are verified against the City of San Gabriel Master Fee Schedule FY 2026-27 (updated June 2, 2026) as of October 25, 2026, unless marked otherwise.

San Gabriel ADU fees come with a puzzle most cities do not pose: the schedule is long, precise, and full of ADU-adjacent numbers — and it still does not price the permit. An 800 sq ft detached ADU here lands at **$303,000, or $379 per square foot** — a floor, not a total. It counts the series-baseline construction phases, $14,000 of design, and $875 of fixed schedule charges. Everything that would normally dominate a fee discussion — the permit, the plan check, the school fee, the impact fees — is excluded, each for a different stated reason, and each marked "verify with city" or "verify with district" below.

One line is deliberately absent. A verified floor total for a 2,000 sq ft single-family new construction cannot be stated from official sources: fee schedules price permits and fees, not construction, so no such figure is verified here and none is estimated. The 2,000 sq ft figure matters only as the proportionality denominator — an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% of the primary's size, the fraction state law uses for impact fees at 750 sq ft and above.

## The schedule that prices everything except the permit

San Gabriel's building-fee section works by reference. Plan check and inspection are set at 140% of LA County fees. Plan check alone is 85% of the permit fee. Fire plan check is 15% of plan check. The general plan fee adds 5% and the technology fee 3%. Every line is exact, and every line needs a base number the schedule never prints — the county table. A schedule that expresses its main price as a percentage of another jurisdiction's unpublished-here table cannot be turned into a dollar permit figure without that table, and importing another city's permit figure would be fabrication. So the permit line is excluded, and the $875 below is everything fixed that the schedule does state.

The same schedule is more forthcoming elsewhere. Its public works section publishes a full development impact fee table, including lines explicitly labeled for a second unit. That section, not the building section, is where San Gabriel's ADU story actually lives.

## The "2nd unit" lines — and why they are not the ADU bill

The schedule's development impact fee section (FY 2026-27, with rates adjusting each August 1 by the change in a construction index) publishes these residential figures:

| Published line | Amount | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Police facility, residential | $1,129.91 per unit | Full per-unit input, not an ADU amount |
| Fire facility, residential | $340.43 per unit | Full per-unit input, not an ADU amount |
| Open space & recreation, residential | $4,244.86 per unit | Full per-unit input, not an ADU amount |
| Traffic, 2nd unit (6 trips) | $2,066.79 per unit | Labeled for a second unit — ADU treatment unstated |
| Sanitary sewer, 2nd unit (1/2 of R-2) | $1,490.46 per unit | Labeled for a second unit — ADU amount unstated |
| Traffic, new SFR in R-1 (10 trips) | $3,444.64 per unit | New-house comparator, not an ADU charge |
| Sanitary sewer, R-1 single-family | $5,949.92 per unit | New-house comparator, not an ADU charge |

Adding the four impact lines gives $7,781.99 — a tempting total that would be wrong to use. It sums full per-unit inputs, while state law requires something else for ADUs.

> **The ADU impact-fee box.** California law bars any impact fee on an ADU under 750 square feet, and requires proportional charges at 750 square feet or more (Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A)). Applied to San Gabriel's published lines as an illustration only: an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% of the primary's size, so 40% of $7,781.99 would be about $3,113 — if the city applies proportionality to these lines. The schedule does not say it does, and the traffic and sewer lines are labeled "2nd unit" rather than "ADU," so the illustration is not a bill and is not in the total. The sewer line carries a second caveat: connection and capacity charges are not "impact fees" under the statute, and an ADU is not a new residential use for those charges unless built with a new single-family dwelling (§65852.2(f)(2)).

## What is missing, in one place

Three gaps, each with a different owner. The permit gap belongs to the county table: San Gabriel's schedule cannot be completed without the LA County fee table it references. The impact-fee gap belongs to the city's ADU policy: the schedule publishes second-unit lines but never says the word ADU, so whether those lines are charged in full, proportionally, or not at all is a question only the city can answer. The school-fee gap belongs to the district: no adopted rate was located, and the state ceiling is not a substitute. A post that filled any of these three gaps with another jurisdiction's number would look more complete and be less honest.
