West Covina is the first three-school-district city in this series — and that single fact does more to your budget than anything in the city's fee schedule. Depending on which district boundary your address falls in, the school fee on the same 800 sq ft ADU is **$3,064, $3,264, or $4,136**: a $1,072 swing decided by geography, not design. Our six-phase total lands at **$314,000, or $393 per square foot** — just under the LA County baseline — using West Covina Unified's rate for the majority of the city.

Around that centerpiece sits a profile of middle-of-the-pack numbers and one unusually strict rule. The 2026 development impact fee is **$4,845.86 per single-family detached unit**, charged to ADUs proportionately against the primary dwelling's square footage (~$2,584 against a 1,500 sq ft house — an assumption we mark). The building permit is valuation-based with no published ADU figure, so we carry a worked example (~$5,672) built on an assumed valuation. And West Covina is one of the strictest cities in this series on ADU utilities: the ordinance mandates shared connections — no separate meters, no separate addresses — making sewer and water a **$0** line in the standard case. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; West Covina does not publish phase-level construction costs. HCD logged 374 ADU permits here in 2022–2024, 42nd statewide.

## The school fee depends on your address

This is the line to read twice before you budget. West Covina is served by three school districts, and each charges its own residential developer fee:

- **West Covina USD** (majority of the city): **$3.83/sq ft** → **$3,064** on 800 sq ft, from the district's official FY 2024-25 annual report. This is the figure in our breakdown.
- **Rowland USD** (southern West Covina — the district's own developer-fee page says fees are collected "before the building permit is issued by... the City of West Covina"): **$5.17/sq ft** → **$4,136**. The page may be stale: $5.17 matches the pre-2026 statutory maximum.
- **Covina-Valley USD** (northern West Covina): **$4.08/sq ft** → **$3,264** — but this comes from the FY 2022-23 developer fee report, the oldest figure in the set; the current rate was not found.

The swing between the low and high district is **$1,072** on the same ADU — larger than the DIF itself. Every rate here is marked partial: none of the three districts has a published FY 2025-26 figure, and whether any adopted the State Allocation Board's 2026 maximum of $5.38 is unconfirmed. One correction against sloppy research: Baldwin Park USD's $3.48/sq ft sometimes appears in West Covina aggregations, but no official source places any part of West Covina inside BPUSD boundaries — it is a neighboring district only, and we do not count it.

## The proportionate DIF: (800 ÷ your house) × $4,845.86

West Covina's 2026 Master Fee Schedule (Resolution 2024-50, CPI-adjusted, effective January 1, 2026) sets the development impact fee at **$4,845.86 per single-family detached unit**: police $882.90, fire $1,076.74, parks $2,652.97, city administration $155.49, public works $77.75. The schedule has no ADU-specific line — the ADU pays its share by state law, proportionate to the primary dwelling's square footage: (800 ÷ primary sq ft) × $4,845.86. Against a 1,500 sq ft house that is **~$2,584**; against a 2,000 sq ft house it is ~$1,938; against a 1,000 sq ft house it is ~$3,877. The primary dwelling's size is the lever — which is exactly why our total carries the assumption in the open. Notably absent from the city code is any local 750 sq ft rule: we searched the full 6,075-line Ordinance 2480 packet for "750" and found no match. The ordinance was adopted to be "consistent with the State," and the state baseline governs — under 750 sq ft, no impact fees; at 750 and above, proportional (Gov. Code 66324(c)(1)).

## $0 sewer and water — by ordinance, not by luck

West Covina runs one of the stricter ADU utility rules in this series. The ADU ordinance (Ordinance 2480, WCMC Ch. 26) **requires ADUs and JADUs to share utility connections with the primary use — no separate utility meters, no separate addresses**. The city's ADU page states it plainly: ADUs/JADUs "are not allowed to have second addresses or separate utilities from the main residence." State law independently bars connection or capacity fees for a compliant ADU (Gov. Code 66323). So the standard detached ADU on an existing lot pays **$0** in sewer or water connection or capacity fees — not by omission, but by mandate. The one exception: for a new detached ADU that is not a conversion, the ordinance allows the city to require a new or upgraded connection with a proportionate fee — no dollar amount is published for that case. Also note the providers are split four ways for water (Suburban Water Systems, Rowland Water District, San Gabriel Valley Water Company, City of Covina Water), which is where a capacity-fee question would get complicated — but the shared-connection mandate makes it moot in the standard case.

## The permit you can only estimate

The 2026 Master Fee Schedule is confirmed and current — it just can't tell you what an 800 sq ft ADU costs to permit. There is no flat ADU building permit fee and no published $/sq ft valuation table; the fee runs on the Building Official's valuation through published tiers ($1,695.07 + $20.73 per $1,000 over $50,000 in the $50k–$100k band), with plan check at 85% of the permit fee and issuance at $58.60. Our worked example assumes a **$96,000 valuation ($120/sq ft)** — an assumption, not city data: permit $2,648.65 + plan check $2,251.35 + issuance $58.60 + a 17.70% General Plan Update surcharge on the permit fee ($468.81) + a 0.238%-of-valuation planning review surcharge ($228.48) + SMIP ($12.48) + SB 1473 tiers ($4) ≈ **$5,672** all-in. If the Building Official's valuation differs, every line moves. The honest floor is the planning-side application itself: the **Second Unit Review at $1,022.42 for new construction** (confirmed, counted), higher than several peer cities. That fee is payable before the building-side math even starts.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. West Covina's proportionate DIF will need new math — an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 1,500 sq ft house currently owes ~$2,584 on the full 800 sq ft; under SB 1117, the fee applies to 50 sq ft, which requires a per-square-foot DIF rate the current schedule does not publish. The city has no local cliff language to amend — which simplifies compliance and complicates budgeting in equal measure. We found no mention of SB 1117 in city materials; the January 2027 quarterly review will check for a revised fee schedule. School fees are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and stay as billed by each district.
