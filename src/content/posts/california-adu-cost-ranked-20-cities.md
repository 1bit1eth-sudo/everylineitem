Twenty cities. Same 800 sq ft detached ADU. Same mid-grade finishes. **$44,000 apart** on paper — from Salinas (~$300,000*) to San Diego ($344,000).

But read the asterisks first. Among the 15 cities with **firm totals**, the spread is exactly what [Day 25's table](/posts/california-adu-cost-ranked-10-cities/) found: **about $12,000** — Long Beach ($313,000) to San Francisco ($325,000), with San Diego ($344,000) still the outlier. The headline widening comes from five preliminary totals, each marked honestly. The thesis hasn't changed: the spread lives in Phase 1 — the soft costs. Construction barely moves between these cities.

## What changed since Day 25

**Ten cities added.** The Day 25 table ranked 10; this edition doubles it. The biggest movers in the story:

- **The floor dropped.** Santa Maria ($306,000*) was already the nominal low in v1 but excluded for unverified items. Now five asterisked preliminary entries reach lower — Salinas at ~$300,000* on the back of a full DIF waiver, and Palo Alto at ~$303,000 after correcting a widely-circulated bad number.
- **The Palo Alto correction.** Batch-matrix research caught a real error: DIF was rumored at ~$76,000 and permits at 5.42%. Both wrong. DIF is ~$8,000–$20,000; permits are 1.76% (~$5,790). The corrected total is a **series-low candidate**, not the series high it was assumed to be.
- **San Diego is still the outlier.** At $344,000, it sits $19,000 above San Francisco — more than the entire rest of the firm spread combined. Its soft-cost stack, not its construction cost, explains the gap.

## What the spread tells you

**Among firm totals, $12,000 is the story — and it's small.** Under 4% of a $320,000 build. Nobody should pick a city for ADU fees alone. But $12,000 is also real money, and it concentrates in lines you can plan around:

- **School fees** are the most predictable spread: $1,792 (Pasadena) to ~$4,320 (Campbell). District boundaries, not city boundaries, set this number.
- **Impact fees** are the most volatile: $0 (Salinas, Oceanside, Oakland) to ~$10,411 (Anaheim's DIF).
- **Permit fees** are the least predictable: $1,006 (Riverside) to ~$19,500 (San Francisco). Valuation-based cities punish expensive builds; flat-fee cities don't.
- **The DIF waiver trend is accelerating.** Salinas (full waiver), Oceanside, Fremont, Santa Monica — more cities are dropping DIF to $0 for ADUs. This is the single biggest driver of the new low-end entries.

**The cliff still matters more than the ranking.** Nearly every city in this table has a meaningful 750 sq ft cliff. Shrink the ADU to 749 sq ft and several cities drop $5,000–$11,000 in impact fees alone. The cheapest ADU in California isn't in the cheapest city; it's the 749 sq ft ADU in a high-cliff city.

## How to use this table

1. **Find your city** (or nearest published neighbor) for a planning baseline.
2. **Read that city's full post** — the total is the headline; the ledger is the tool.
3. **Check the cliff** before finalizing square footage. The 750 line is worth more than any city choice.
4. **Verify the asterisked items** with the city. Our totals are honest about what's confirmed and what isn't.
