Most California cities charge ADU impact fees **per unit**: one flat number, due at the permit counter. Redlands charges **per square foot** — $8.7391 for every square foot of the building. That one design choice turns the state's 750 sq ft threshold into the steepest cliff in our [fee index](/fee-index/).

Here is why. State law lets cities exempt ADUs of 750 sq ft or less from impact fees, and Redlands' Ordinance No. 2968 does exactly that: its definition of "Residential Property" includes an "Accessory Dwelling Unit **above 750 sq.ft.**" — and nothing smaller. At 750 sq ft your impact-fee bill is **$0**. At 751 sq ft the rate applies to the *entire* unit, not the extra foot: 751 × $8.7391 ≈ **$6,563**. The 751st square foot is the most expensive square foot in this series — it costs more than most cities' entire fee stack.

This is a fee autopsy, not a full build budget: construction phases are priced in the city deep-dives. What Redlands takes before you pour a footing is the subject here — and $6,991.28 of it is confirmed to the cent.

## The cliff, line by line

Ordinance No. 2968 (adopted July 16, 2024, effective January 1, 2025) sets eleven per-square-foot lines. On an 800 sq ft detached ADU, all eleven apply:

| DIF line (Redlands Municipal Code) | Rate / sq ft | 800 sq ft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water Capital Improvement (Ch. 3.48) | $2.3657 | $1,892.56 |
| Transportation System Improvements (Ch. 3.54) | $1.3954 | $1,116.32 |
| Open Space and Parks (Ch. 3.32) | $1.1794 | $943.52 |
| Public Facilities (Ch. 3.60) | $0.7145 | $571.60 |
| Acquisition of Water Stocks and Water Rights (Ch. 13.40) | $0.6633 | $530.64 |
| Library | $0.5997 | $479.76 |
| Police Facilities | $0.5197 | $415.76 |
| Storm Drain Facilities (Ch. 3.56) | $0.5113 | $409.04 |
| Fire Protection Facilities | $0.4503 | $360.24 |
| Sewer Capital Improvement (Ch. 3.44) | $0.2696 | $215.68 |
| Solid Waste Capital Improvement (Ch. 3.70) | $0.0702 | $56.16 |
| **Total** | **$8.7391** | **$6,991.28** |

Two carve-outs matter. First, garage conversions are treated differently: the sewer capital, water capital, and water stocks/rights lines do not apply to an ADU created inside an existing structure. Second, the ordinance has **no automatic escalator** — these amounts change only by a new council action, which is why a 2024-adopted schedule can still be quoted as current.

## A third of it is water

Add the three utility lines — Water Capital ($2.3657), Water Stocks and Water Rights ($0.6633), Sewer Capital ($0.2696) — and you get **$3.2986 of the $8.7391 total: 37.7% of the stack is water**. Redlands runs its own water and sewer utilities (Municipal Utilities & Engineering Department; no special district), and the "Water Stocks and Water Rights" line is a piece of local history — a charge that funds buying up water rights and stock as new development arrives.

Hold onto that 37.7%. It decides what happens in 2027.

## The school fee: $3,832, with an asterisk

Redlands Unified is a unified K–12 district, so there is no elementary/high-school split: one Level I rate covers the parcel. The last rate we can confirm from an adopted resolution is **$4.79/sq ft** (Resolution No. 7, 2022-23) — **$3,832** on 800 sq ft.

The asterisk: RUSD prepared a 2024 School Facilities Fee Justification Report at the state's then-maximum of **$5.17/sq ft**, and its board records show a hearing on adopting it. We could not find the adopted resolution in this pass. So budget $3,832, know that $4,136 (at $5.17) is possible, and treat any quote above that as unproven. School fees also answer to a different threshold than the city's — the state 500 sq ft assessable-space rule, not the 750 sq ft cliff — and an 800 sq ft ADU is over both.

## The permit: $7,861, dated December 2023

Redlands prices building permits **per square foot by construction type** (Resolution No. 8522, December 2023). For a standard wood-frame (Type VA/VB) R-3 unit, an 800 sq ft ADU lands in the 1,000 sq ft permit tier at **$4,137**, plus plan check at **$3,724** — **$7,861** together, before small schedule add-ons (permit issuance, general plan maintenance at $0.16/sq ft, a 2% technology surcharge, and flat MEP unit fees).

The caveat is printed in the resolution itself: the Finance Director may raise these fees every July by CPI. Whether the 2024, 2025, and 2026 adjustments were applied — and to what — we could not confirm. Budget $7,861 as a floor from the last published schedule, not as a quote.

## What we could not confirm

Honesty section, because this site labels what it cannot prove:

- **Planning fee:** none found. There is no ADU line item in the current fee schedule, and Redlands processes ADUs ministerially. We did not find a document that says "the ADU planning fee is $0" — only the absence of a charge.
- **Pre-approved plans:** the City Council directed a free standard-plans program (RRM Design Group; four styles, studio to 2-bedroom) and funded the design contract. Whether the finished plans are posted and downloadable today is unverified.
- **Separate meter/lateral charges:** ADUs connect to the city's existing utilities; beyond the capital lines inside the DIF, no separate flat ADU connection charge was found in official sources.

Each of these is on our December re-verification list.

## A note on SB 1117

From January 1, 2027, [SB 1117](/posts/sb-1117-kills-750-sq-ft-cliff/) limits covered impact fees on up to two ADUs to the square footage **above** 750. In most cities that nearly erases the DIF. In Redlands it erases only the covered slice:

| Redlands DIF on an 800 sq ft ADU | 2026 | Jan 1, 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Covered lines ($5.4405/sq ft) | $4,352.40 (800 sq ft basis) | $272.03 (50 sq ft basis) |
| Water/sewer capital lines ($3.2986/sq ft) | $2,638.88 | $2,638.88 — excluded by §66311.5(c)(2) |
| **Total city DIF** | **$6,991.28** | **$2,910.91** |

The school fee ($3,832) is not a covered impact fee and does not move. So yes, the cliff collapses in 2027 — but Redlands keeps one of the largest post-2027 residuals in our matrix, precisely because more than a third of its stack was never an "impact fee" in the statute's sense. It was water.

If you are choosing between a December 2026 permit and a January 2027 one in Redlands, that $4,080.37 difference is the number to argue about with your calendar.
