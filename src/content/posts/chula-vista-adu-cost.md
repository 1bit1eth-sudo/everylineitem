Chula Vista prices ADU permits like a menu, not a formula. Every other city in this series runs the building permit through a valuation schedule — dollars per thousand of assumed construction value, then plan-check percentages on top of that. Chula Vista's Fee Bulletin 10-400 just says: detached ADU, standard plan, **$3,951**. That is $476 intake + $533 plan check + $2,942 inspection, published in one line, no math required. It is the only flat-rate ADU permit in the series, and the most transparently priced permit we have published.

The flat rate pairs with the 12 pre-approved City Standard ADU Plans the city built with SnapADU — every one already through plan check. Use one of those plans and the permit is $3,951; draw your own detached design and it is $4,963, also flat. The menu tells you exactly what each choice costs before you start.

Our six-phase total lands at **$308,000, or $385 per square foot** — well under the LA County baseline, though with honest gaps: the DIF dollar amount (proportional to the primary residence, with no published base figure) and ADU sewer/water capacity charges are marked "verify with city" and excluded from the total. The school fee is only half confirmed — the high-school district's $3.01 rate is verified, the elementary district's rate could not be found. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Chula Vista does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## The flat-rate permit: the series standout

In every other city we have published, the building permit is a function of valuation — the city assumes what the project costs, then applies a fee tier. Torrance's flat-rate DIF ($2,243.47) was a series first for impact fees; Chula Vista does the same trick for the permit itself. $3,951 for a detached ADU on a Standard Plan, $4,963 for a custom detached design, and tiered per-300-SF pricing for attached units — all flat, all printed in the bulletin. No one has to guess the valuation.

The practical effect is budget certainty at the counter. A homeowner walks in with a chosen Standard Plan and knows the permit line to the dollar before filing. Compare that with Burbank's valuation-based permit (the series' most expensive permit-plus-plan-check at $3,692) or Long Beach's DIF uncertainty, and Chula Vista's approach reads as deliberate: pair pre-approved plans with a flat permit to pull friction out of the process.

## The 750 sq ft cliff: proportional, not flat

Under 750 square feet, Chula Vista's DIF treatment is simple — total exemption for the ADU and any JADU, straight from Fee Bulletin 16-100 (October 2025). At 750 and above, the city charges the ADU proportionally to the primary residence by square footage: the legal formula is published, but the city does not publish the base dwelling fee it multiplies against, so there is no dollar figure we could verify. Our 800 sq ft example crosses the cliff and would owe proportional DIF — we show the formula and the exemption boundary, not a number, and excluded it from the total. This is one of the items to settle with the city.

## $2,408: the half-confirmed school fee

The Sweetwater Union High School District rate is solid — **$3.01 per square foot**, effective June 22, 2026 after an April 20, 2026 board hearing, published on the district's own developer-fees page. On 800 sq ft that is **$2,408**. (Within part of Sweetwater's boundary, the San Ysidro High School area uses $2.10 per square foot instead.)

The gap is the other half of the student's life: the Chula Vista Elementary School District (K–6). We could not find any published developer-fee rate for CVESD — no dollar-per-square-foot figure, no adopted resolution. That means $2,408 is a floor, not a total: the true school fee for an ADU in Chula Vista may be higher once the elementary portion is added. Until we have the CVESD number, the fee autopsy carries an explicit blank.

## Pre-approved plans that make the flat permit real

Twelve City Standard ADU Plans — six designs plus their mirror images — built with SnapADU, each already through plan check for code compliance. This is the most plans offered pre-approved in the series (Garden Grove's ADU Go and Anaheim's ADU Express each offer four), and it is load-bearing for the fee story: the $3,951 flat permit is specifically the "Standard (All Plans)" rate, unlocked by using one of these designs. A custom detached design is $4,963, still flat, still published. Design cost itself stays at the series baseline $14,000 — the free plans are the alternative, not the assumption.

## Sewer and water: the blanks we could not fill

Two hard numbers exist and one useful absence does not. The City's sewer capacity charge is confirmed at **$5,072 per EDU** (Fee Bulletin 12-100, October 2025) — but the schedule never says whether an ADU triggers a new EDU, so the application is unconfirmed. For water, there is nothing current at all: only a 2014 Otay Water District rate table surfaced, and service areas split between Sweetwater Authority, California American, and Otay Water depending on the address. Both lines are excluded from the total and marked "verify with city."

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Chula Vista's proportional formula ("proportionally to the primary residence, on a square footage basis") cannot survive that math unchanged — an 800 sq ft ADU would owe fees on 50 sq ft, not 800, which changes both the base and the ratio. We found no mention of SB 1117 in city materials; the January 2027 quarterly review will check for a revised fee bulletin. School fees are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and stay at $2,408 plus whatever CVESD charges.
