Montebello's $302,330 is the second-thinnest total in this batch — and that thinness is the point. One $330 planning line sits on top of the series construction baseline, while the school fee that usually dominates the stack is missing for a specific, documented reason: the district was mid-adjustment when this post was researched. Treat this post as a starting line, not a budget.

The one verified line is the **Accessory Dwelling Unit Review fee of $330**, item 32 of the City of Montebello Planning and Code Fee Schedule approved August 2025. The schedule was adopted as Resolution 25-77 on August 27, 2025, effective November 1, 2025, and the review fee holds flat at $330 through FY 2027-28.

The school fee's absence has a paper trail. On April 13, 2026, Montebello Unified School District sent MGT Impact Solutions a notice of intent to proceed with RFP No. 04 (2025-2026), Developer Fee Adjustment Services, district-wide — the district was buying a fee study, not publishing a fee. No adopted rate, justification study, or district fee page surfaced in this round of research, so the school line stays blank rather than estimated. It is the largest missing piece: when the district publishes its adjusted rate, this total will move by several thousand dollars.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the only counted city fee is the $330 review fee, a planning line, not an impact fee.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The MUSD adopted rate.** The district's fee adjustment was in progress in April 2026. Its published rate, when it lands, is the single largest addition to this floor.
2. **The building permit.** A 0.50%-of-valuation formula needs a valuation basis to become a figure. The city's Building and Safety Fee Schedule (effective November 1, 2025) is the document to run it from.
3. **The city impact fee question.** Whether Montebello charges a residential development impact fee at all — and how it treats ADUs — was not resolved in this round.
