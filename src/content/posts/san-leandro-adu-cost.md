> **DRAFT — not published.** This post is a floor estimate built only from verified fee lines. Unverified fees are excluded, not estimated. Last verified 27 Oct 2026.

San Leandro's $306,136 is a floor with an unusual shape: the city has deliberately zeroed the lines other cities charge. The Park Impact Fee and the street-improvement fee are waived for all ADUs by council resolution, and the Community Planning Fee is waived for ADU permits by schedule note. What remains counted is a single school fee — and even that one carries a $168 district fork.

The counted school fee is **$4,136**, from the San Leandro Unified School District's posted residential rate of **$5.17 per square foot** × 800 sq ft, corroborated by the city's development-fee handout, which lists $5.17 for both San Leandro Unified and San Lorenzo Unified. But San Lorenzo Unified has since moved: its developer-fee page posts **$5.38 per square foot effective July 4, 2026**, which is $4,304 on 800 sq ft. So the floor uses $4,136 and the San Lorenzo figure is disclosed as a +$168 variant, not hidden. Whether San Leandro Unified itself has adopted the 2026 $5.38 rate is unconfirmed — its district page still posts $5.17 — and that is the first thing to re-check before publication.

The waivers are the story. On July 21, 2025, the City Council adopted **Resolutions 2025-106 and 2025-107**, waiving Park Impact Fees and Impact Fees for Street Improvements for all ADUs, as the city's ADU page states. Separately, note [10] in the adopted 2025-26 building fee schedule states the Community Planning Fee will not be charged for accessory dwelling unit permits. Both are confirmed zeros, and both are why this post's fee stack is thinner than its neighbors' without any missing-data asterisk on those lines.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the impact lines are already $0 by council waiver, and the school fee is untouched by SB 1117.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **San Leandro Unified's 2026 rate.** The district page still posts $5.17 while San Lorenzo Unified has adopted $5.38. If San Leandro Unified follows, the floor rises $168 to $306,304 and the district fork disappears.
2. **The building permit.** The valuation-based schedule was not computed to a figure. Running it for an 800 sq ft ADU would convert the largest missing line into a countable one.
3. **Sewer and water connections.** No verified rate was found. The city's ADU cost calculator (via the Alameda County ADU Resource Center, linked from the city ADU page) may carry estimates, but no official connection figure was verified in this round.

## How San Leandro compares

Only published posts are used here. Published Bay Area totals: Palo Alto **~$303,000**, Fremont **$314,000**, Campbell **~$318,000**, Oakland **$322,000**. San Leandro's $306,136 sits near the low end of that band while missing the building permit entirely; once the permit is counted, expect it to land mid-band. The waived impact fees are real savings relative to cities that charge them — but they are savings against an incomplete total, not a completed one.

It does not claim the permit is cheap — it claims the valuation-based schedule was not computed. It does not claim sewer and water cost $0 — it claims no verified rate was found.
