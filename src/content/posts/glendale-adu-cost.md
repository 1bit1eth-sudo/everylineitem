Glendale wrote the most interesting ADU ordinance in this series — and then made the math almost impossible to do by hand.

The ordinance does two things at once. First, it caps detached new-construction ADUs at **800 square feet** — the exact size this series models. Second, it exempts ADUs **under 750 square feet** from all development impact fees. Put those together and you get the series' tightest squeeze: the largest ADU Glendale allows (800 sq ft) sits exactly 50 square feet above the fee cliff. Build at 749 and the impact-fee line is $0. Build at the 800 maximum and it can reach $4,700. There is no size in between that avoids the question.

So this post does two things. First, the standard six-phase breakdown for an 800 sq ft detached ADU in Glendale — **$318,000, or $398 per square foot.** Then the part nobody publishes: an autopsy of the soft-cost line, fee by fee, from the city's own schedule and ordinance — including the one line (the building permit) that the city prices by valuation but never tells you the valuation.

## The fee autopsy: $30,000, line by line

**Building permit: ≈ $2,757 (worked example — read the caveat).** The [FY 2026-27 fee schedule](https://www.glendaleca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/83558/639173905969900000) (effective 8/22/2026) prices the $100,001–$500,000 valuation tier at **$1,756 plus $4.55 per additional $1,000**. What the schedule does *not* publish is the residential construction valuation table — the $/sq ft figure the building department actually multiplies by 800. We work the example at the $320,000 build valuation: $1,756 + 220 × $4.55 = **$2,756.60**. That is conservative — declared valuations typically run below contract price — and the real number moves with whatever valuation the plan checker assigns. If your valuation lands in a different tier, redo this line; the formula is public even when the input is not.

**Plan check: 140% of the permit, plus a 50% surcharge.** Four departments each take a percentage of the building permit fee ([FY 2026-27 schedule](https://www.glendaleca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/83558/639173905969900000)): Building **115%**, Planning **5%**, Public Works **15%**, GWP **5%** — **140% combined**, or about **$3,860** on our worked permit. Then the Permit Program Surcharge adds **50% of plan check** (another ≈ **$1,930**). All-in, the permit counter approaches **$8,550** before a single impact fee — the heaviest review load in the series, driven by multiplier stacking rather than high base rates.

**Development impact fees: $0 – $4,700 (the 50 sq ft cliff).** The [ADU ordinance](https://glendaleca.primegov.com/meeting/attachment/7258.pdf?name=CC_11012022_Ordinance_9a1) (Section D.16) is explicit: ADUs **under 750 sq ft are exempt from any development impact fee**; ADUs of **750 sq ft or greater** pay a Parks and Library fee **proportional to the main dwelling's square footage**, capped at the fee-resolution amount — **$4,700** per the FY 2026-27 schedule's ADU row. An 800 sq ft ADU always triggers it; the exact amount depends on your main house. We carry the midpoint ($2,350) in the soft-cost total and the full range everywhere else. One open question: whether the separate Quimby fee ($19,795 single-family) also applies to 750+ sq ft ADUs — the ordinance names only the Parks and Library fee, and we could not confirm Quimby's treatment from public documents.

**School fees: $4,304 — tied for the series high.** Glendale Unified School District charges **$5.38 per square foot** on ADUs of 500 square feet or more ([GUSD developer fees](https://gusd.net/8515_3)): 800 × $5.38 = **$4,304**. The city ordinance (D.15) confirms the owner pays school-district fees. Note the two cliffs sit at different sizes: school fees switch on at 500 sq ft, impact fees at 750. An ADU between 500 and 750 pays school fees only; at 800, it pays both.

**Water and sewer: $0, in writing.** The ordinance (Sections D.17–20) states that an ADU **"shall not be considered a new residential use for purposes of calculating connection fees or capacity charges** for utilities, including water and sewer" — unless built together with a new single-family dwelling. Share the existing service and the connection-fee line is **$0** by ordinance, not by practice. (A separate meter or new connection can still be charged proportionally under Gov. Code §66013, but that is a choice, not the default.)

**ADU review and covenant: $1,005.** Planning's ADU review runs **$475** and the required Covenant & Agreement recording runs **$530** (both FY 2026-27). Small lines, but they are pure Glendale — not every city itemizes them.

**Design and engineering: $14,000 (carried, with relief available).** The series-standard design line. Glendale operates a [Standard ADU Plan Program](https://glendaleca.gov/government/departments/community-development/development-services/adu) — pre-approved Standard Plans 1, 2, and 3 for expeditious review — but the plans are owned by private firms and sold, not free, so we keep the full line in the model. Note the elegant detail: Standard Plan 1 is **740 sq ft**, which slides under the 750 sq ft impact-fee exemption. The program's own flagship design appears to dodge the city's own cliff.

## The 50 square foot cliff

| Fee at 800 sq ft | Glendale | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parks and Library DIF | **$0 – $4,700** | Main-house proportional, capped |
| Quimby fee | **Unconfirmed** | Ordinance silent; not in total |
| School district developer fees | **$4,304** | GUSD, unaffected by the 750 rule |
| Water / sewer capacity charges | **$0** | Ordinance: not a new residential use |

Glendale's cliff is the narrowest in the series and the most unavoidable. In San Diego the cliff spans hundreds of square feet of design freedom; in Glendale it is 50. The ordinance allows 800 and exempts 749 — there is no compliant detached design between the exemption and the maximum that meaningfully changes the fee outcome. If you want the DIF line at $0, you build at 749 or below; at 800, you pay whatever the main-house proportion yields, up to $4,700.

Starting 2027-01-01, [SB 1117](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB1117) changes the statewide rule so impact fees on the first two ADUs apply only to square footage *above* 750 — which would shrink Glendale's already-narrow cliff to the 50 sq ft sliver itself. The city's 2022 ordinance predates the law and will need updating; flagged for the December quarterly re-verification.

## What moves the number most in Glendale

**1. The valuation the plan checker assigns.** The $8,550 permit-and-review figure swings directly with valuation — and Glendale does not publish the $/sq ft table. A lower assigned valuation drops the permit, the 140% plan check, and the 50% surcharge together. Ask Building & Safety how they value detached ADUs before you budget; the formula is public, the input is a conversation.

**2. Your main house's size.** The DIF line is $0–$4,700 entirely as a function of primary-dwelling square footage. Two identical 800 sq ft ADUs on different lots can owe different impact fees. Get the proportion from the fee resolution before finalizing.

**3. The school fee.** At $4,304 it is the largest single fee line and there is no design-around above 500 sq ft. Budget it first.

**4. Whether you use a Standard Plan.** The 740 sq ft flagship plan is the only pre-approved design in the series that structurally avoids its own city's cliff. The time savings are real (expeditious review); the fee savings ($0 DIF) are arithmetic.

**5. Meter strategy.** $0 on shared existing service versus proportional charges on a new connection. Decide before design.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does an ADU cost in Glendale in 2026?**

About $318,000 for an 800 sq ft detached, single-story, new-construction unit with mid-grade finishes in Glendale — roughly $398 per square foot. That is our modeled median: the soft-cost phase is rebuilt from the city's official fee schedule and ADU ordinance, while construction phases are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline because Glendale does not publish phase-level construction costs.

**Does Glendale charge impact fees on ADUs?**

Only at 750 square feet and above. Under 750, ADUs are fully exempt; at 800, a Parks and Library fee applies in proportion to the main house, capped at $4,700. Because the ordinance caps detached ADUs at 800 sq ft, the maximum-size ADU always pays.

**How much are ADU permit fees in Glendale?**

It depends on the valuation the city assigns — the rate tiers are public but the $/sq ft valuation table is not. Worked at the $320,000 build valuation, the building permit is about $2,757, plan check across four departments adds 140% (about $3,860), and the program surcharge adds another 50% of plan check (about $1,930).

**Do I pay school district fees on an ADU in Glendale?**

Yes — $4,304 on 800 sq ft, tied with Long Beach for the highest in the series. GUSD charges $5.38 per square foot on ADUs of 500 square feet or more.

**Does Glendale have pre-approved ADU plans?**

Yes — the Standard ADU Plan Program offers pre-approved Plans 1, 2, and 3 for expeditious review. Plans are sold by their private designers, not free. Notably, Plan 1 at 740 sq ft falls under the 750 sq ft impact-fee exemption.
