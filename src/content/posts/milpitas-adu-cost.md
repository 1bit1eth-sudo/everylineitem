Milpitas is the strongest total in this batch, because the city publishes the one document every ADU builder wishes every city published: a permit fee estimate table with an 800 square foot row. Add the school district's rate and one flat city fee, and the floor stands on three verified legs instead of one. The remaining gap is the utility connection bill, which Milpitas prices as a proportion of the main house — so it cannot be fixed without a parcel.

The permit number comes straight from the City of Milpitas Office of Building Safety's ADU Permit Fee Estimate, based on the FY25-26 Fee Schedule. The 800 square foot row reads: plan review $1,915.53, inspection $2,529.59, miscellaneous $436.26, planning plan review $142.73 minimum, land development plan review $231 minimum — total **$5,255.11 minimum**. The planning and land development lines are hourly minimums (planning at $275/hour plus fees, land development at $231/hour plus fees), so treat $5,255.11 as a floor within the floor. The same handout adds a **$709 new-address request permit fee** — a separate address is required for a new ADU — and notes a $703 fire department review may apply if sprinklers are required. The $709 is counted; the $703 is conditional and is not.

The school line is Milpitas Unified School District at **$5.17 per square foot**, adopted March 12, 2024 and effective March 13, 2024. The district's rule for ADUs is explicit: at 500 square feet or more of assessable space, fees are charged on the entire amount. On 800 square feet that is **$4,136**. One staleness flag: the state maximum rose to $5.38 in January 2026, and whether Milpitas Unified has adopted it is unconfirmed. At $5.38 the school line would be $4,304.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the permit estimate and address fee are service fees, not impact fees, and the connection formulas are capacity charges.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The connection bill.** For illustration only, on a lot with a 1,600 sq ft main residence, the three proportional formulas would total ($1,910 + $1,908 + $880) × (800 ÷ 1,600) = $2,349. On a smaller main house the bill is larger; on a larger one, smaller. It is not in the total because the benchmark fixes only the ADU's size.
2. **The November 2, 2026 fee schedule.** The city has published a Fee Schedule effective November 2, 2026 — after this post's verification date. The permit estimate above uses the FY25-26 schedule; recheck the 800 square foot row once the new schedule takes effect.
3. **The MUSD rate's currency.** $5.17 is a March 2024 adoption. A 2026 district adoption at the $5.38 state maximum would add $168 to the school line alone.
