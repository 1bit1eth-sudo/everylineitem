Three cities. One county. The same 800 sq ft detached ADU on paper — and a **$36,000** spread in what it costs, decided almost entirely by fee schedules.

This is the fourth regional comparison in the series (after LA County, Orange County, and the Bay Area), and San Diego County may be the most instructive: the three cities below sit within one metro area, share the same building code, and price the permit counter like three different planets.

## The ranking

| | Chula Vista | Oceanside | City of San Diego |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Total (800 sq ft)** | **$308,000** | **$313,000** | **$344,000** |
| Per sq ft | $385 | $392 | $430 |
| Building permit + plan check | **$3,951 (flat)** | **~$7,541\*** | **~$16,500** |
| School fee | $2,408 (HSD only, half-confirmed) | $3,832 (confirmed) | series baseline carried |
| Impact / DIF | Pro-rata to house — **amount not published** | **$0 (ordinance)** — draft would add up to $4,824 | ~$5,000–$8,000 at 800 sq ft (our estimate) |
| Sewer / water | Verify with city | Not found — verify with city | carried |
| 750 sq ft cliff | Pro-rata DIF switches on at 750 | No cliff (ordinance says $0) | ~$5,000–$8,000 cliff (our estimate) |
| Pre-approved plans | 12 (SnapADU partnership) | 0 (program exists, library empty) | Accepts County / Chula Vista / Encinitas plans |

\*Oceanside's $7,541 is partial — priced from 2018 and 2021 rate tables, with the ADU's fee classification unconfirmed. Full footnotes: [Chula Vista](/posts/chula-vista-adu-cost/) · [Oceanside](/posts/oceanside-adu-cost/) · [San Diego](/posts/san-diego-adu-cost/).

The $36,000 spread between Chula Vista and the City of San Diego is the whole argument for checking the jurisdiction, not the zip code.

## Three philosophies

**Chula Vista: the menu.** Every other city runs the permit through valuation math — assumed construction value, tiered fee schedules, percentages on percentages. Chula Vista's Fee Bulletin 10-400 just lists a flat **$3,951** for a detached ADU on one of its 12 pre-approved SnapADU plans ($476 intake + $533 plan check + $2,942 inspection), $4,963 for a custom design. No formula, no guessing. It is the most transparently priced permit in the series — and the city pairs it with the series' largest pre-approved plan library, so the menu is load-bearing, not decorative.

The caveat is the size of what the menu does not price: the DIF dollar amount (proportional to the primary residence, base figure unpublished), the elementary school district fee (could not be found), and sewer/water capacity charges are all "verify with city" and excluded from the $308,000. Chula Vista's total is the least complete in this ranking — and still the cheapest.

**Oceanside: the contradiction.** The zoning ordinance says, plainly, "The City shall not impose any impact fees upon the development of an ADU or JADU." Oakland reached $0 by exemption; Salinas by waiver; Oceanside by flat declaration. But the city's own August 2025 park DIF nexus draft — unadopted, still a draft — justifies up to **$6.03/sq ft** and exempts only ADUs under 750 sq ft. On 800 sq ft that is up to **$4,824** the ordinance says does not exist. We priced the post on the ordinance: **$0**. If the draft is ever adopted, Oceanside's ranking row moves $4,824 in the wrong direction overnight.

Oceanside also carries the series' steepest permit bill at ~$7,541 — square-foot-based pricing (plan check + inspection + fire and water review + surcharges), more than seven times Riverside's $1,006. Three asterisks apply: the tables are old vintages, the ADU's fee classification is unconfirmed, and the draft's fate is unknown.

**City of San Diego: the heavyweight.** $344,000, or $430 per square foot — about 7% above the LA County baseline, most of it subcontractor rates rather than fees. But the fee philosophy is the story: plan check ($8,085.26) plus inspection ($8,401.90) at the single-dwelling rate — **~$16,500** — the kind of line that makes a $17,400 swing possible between the city and Encinitas on identical drawings. The 750 sq ft cliff bites here too: crossing 750 switches on roughly $5,000–$8,000 in impact fees (our estimate), making the last 50 sq ft cost $530–$590 per foot.

The city's softening answer is not its own plan library — it runs none — but **accepted pre-approved plans** from the County, Chula Vista, and Encinitas programs with a 30-day review. If one fits your lot, most of the $14,000 design line disappears.

## Why the spread

Three variables explain nearly all of it:

1. **Permit pricing model.** Flat menu (Chula Vista), square-footage-based (Oceanside), or valuation-at-single-dwelling-rate (San Diego). The model matters more than the city — a $12,500 range from one modeling choice.
2. **The DIF stance.** Pro-rata but unpublished (Chula Vista), declared $0 with a draft looming (Oceanside), or full cliff (San Diego).
3. **Pre-approved plans.** Twelve free designs (Chula Vista), an empty library (Oceanside), or borrowed from neighbors with a 30-day review (San Diego).

Construction phases 2–6 are nearly identical across all three — carried from the LA County baseline at Chula Vista and Oceanside, modeled at $430/sq ft in San Diego on slightly tighter trade rates. Systems and interior is the largest line everywhere, ~46–47% of the build. The houses are the same. The counters are different.

## What changes January 1, 2027

SB 1117's marginal rule — impact fees only on square footage above 750, for a homeowner's first two ADUs — lands differently in each column:

- **City of San Diego:** the ~$5,000–$8,000 cliff collapses toward roughly **$313–$500** — the largest dollar rescue in this ranking. The $16,500 permit bill is untouched: permits are not impact fees.
- **Chula Vista:** the proportional formula ("proportionally to the primary residence, on a square-footage basis") cannot survive marginal math unchanged — an 800 sq ft ADU would owe on 50 sq ft, not 800. The city has published nothing about implementation yet; the January 2027 review checks for a revised bulletin.
- **Oceanside:** if the ordinance's $0 survives, nothing changes. If the park draft is adopted, SB 1117 rewrites its $4,824 full-area charge to about **$302** (marginal on the 50 sq ft above 750).

School fees — Chula Vista's $2,408, Oceanside's $3,832 — are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and stay put. The December-vs-January permit question is live in all three cities: permits pulled in 2026 use the old math.
