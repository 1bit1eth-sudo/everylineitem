Newport Beach is a useful case because the city itself charges nothing in impact fees on an ADU — and that $0 is documented, not assumed. The adopted fee resolution exempts ADUs outright, so the floor's two counted lines both come from other agencies: the school district and the regional sanitation district. What is missing is the city's own building permit, which is priced off valuation tables rather than a flat ADU figure.

The school line is Newport-Mesa Unified School District at **$5.17 per square foot**, from the district's own Developer Fees page and its 2025 Fee Justification Study. On 800 square feet that is **$4,136**. The page notes additions under 500 square feet may be exempt; at 800 square feet the full rate applies. One staleness flag: the state maximum rose to $5.38 in January 2026, and whether Newport-Mesa has adopted it is unconfirmed — at $5.38 the school line would be $4,304.

The sewer line is the Orange County Sanitation District Capital Facilities Capacity Charge. Table A, effective July 1, 2026, states that accessory dwelling units are charged at the average demand rate and the minimum charge does not apply; the average demand rate is **$1,710 per 1,000 square feet or portion thereof**. An 800 sq ft ADU is one portion, so the charge is **$1,710**. Because it is a capacity charge, SB 1117's proration rule does not touch it.

The $0 line matters as much as the counted ones. City of Newport Beach Resolution No. 2024-83 (adopted November 12, 2024) adopted the Development Impact Fee Nexus Study and the FY 2024-25 fee schedule for Recreation, Police, Fire, Water, and Sewer Facilities — and Exhibit B, item 7(a), lists accessory dwelling units and junior accessory dwelling units as exempt from those fees. So the Nexus Study's proportional ADU formula (an 800 sq ft ADU on a 1,600 sq ft lot would pay 50% of the single-family fee) never produces a city bill: the exemption governs.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the city DIF is already $0 by exemption, and the OC San charge is a capacity charge.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The building permit.** The largest unknown. Newport Beach prices permits off valuation tables in its Schedule of Rents, Fines and Fees; no flat ADU figure was located, so this line is entirely uncounted.
2. **The NMUSD rate's currency.** $5.17 is the district's published rate under its 2025 study. A 2026 adoption at the $5.38 state maximum would add $168 to the school line alone.
3. **The OC San billing basis.** Table A bills "per 1,000 square feet or portion thereof," which reads as one $1,710 block for an 800 sq ft ADU. A pro-rata reading would give $1,368 — $342 lower. The block reading is used here because the table's own footnote says calculations are on a 1,000-square-foot-or-portion basis.
