## Three fees homeowners most often get wrong

**1. Borrowing another city's permit number.** Diamond Bar is a contract-city trap in reverse: because so many nearby cities route permits through LA County, it is tempting to carry a county permit figure into a Diamond Bar budget. The city issues its own permits, on its own schedule, with its own surcharge stack — 15.3% plus 6% plus 1% plus 10% on top of an 85% plan check. None of that is a county figure. The schedule's shape is published; the valuation input is not. Until the city supplies a valuation for an 800 sq ft ADU, the permit line is blank, and any number filling it is invented.

**2. Treating the state school-fee ceiling as a district rate.** The $5.38 per sq ft ceiling adopted January 28, 2026, is the maximum a district may charge after adopting it — not the charge. Walnut Valley's last justified figure found is $4.08, years old; Pomona Unified's current rate was not found at all. And the district split itself needs a parcel check: the boundary decides which district's rate applies. Publishing 800 × $5.38 = $4,304 as "the" Diamond Bar school fee would be wrong twice over: wrong rate, and possibly wrong district.

**3. Assuming the DBADU program means free plans and no utility charges.** The program page says neither. Pre-approval confirms compliance in general; it does not waive fees, does not eliminate trade permits, and does not decide the sewer or water question. The DBADU conditions actually point the other way on utilities: pre-approved ADUs "shall be reliant on the existing main residence utilities," which suggests no new separate connection for that program — but that is a program condition, not a water-district fee ruling, and it does not cover a custom detached ADU. The LACSD single-family $1,793 and WVWD $5,630 figures are reference rates for other project types, not ADU charges, and they are not summed into the total.

## The ordinance, in brief

The city's Community Development FAQ, citing Development Code §22.42.120, sets the shape of a Diamond Bar ADU: one ADU plus one JADU on any residentially zoned property regardless of lot size; studio and one-bedroom ADUs up to 850 sq ft; two-or-more-bedroom ADUs up to 1,000 sq ft; JADUs up to 500 sq ft within the house. An 800 sq ft ADU fits under either bedroom-count cap. The municipal code text itself could not be opened in this research (the code host returned an error), so the FAQ summary is the verified city statement, not a code quotation.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 1, 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Diamond Bar publishes no city impact-fee amount, so SB 1117's effect here cannot be quantified — but the direction is the same as everywhere: an 800 sq ft ADU's taxable impact area shrinks from 800 sq ft to 50 sq ft. School fees are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition, and connection/capacity charges are excluded entirely. The January 2027 quarterly review will check for a city valuation table, a district-adopted school rate, and a District No. 21 ADU sewer ruling.
