**DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.** Numbers below are verified against City of Bakersfield, North of the River Recreation and Park District, and school district documents as of November 13, 2026, unless marked otherwise.

Bakersfield ADU fees are the thinnest file in this batch — and that thinness is itself the finding. The city's latest verified master fee schedule dates to 2024, it prices permits from a valuation it never fixes for ADUs, and the school fee splits across non-unified districts — though for the typical Bakersfield City + Kern High parcel the combined total is now pinned at $5.38 per sq ft. An 800 sq ft detached ADU here lands at **$306,000, or $383 per square foot** — a floor, not a total. It counts the series-baseline construction phases, $14,000 of design, and the $4,304 school fee. Everything else a buyer would normally ask about — the permit, the park fee, the sewer charge — is excluded, each for a stated reason, and each marked "verify with city" or "verify with district" below.

One line is deliberately absent. A verified floor total for a 2,000 sq ft single-family new construction cannot be stated from official sources: fee schedules price permits and fees, not construction, so no such figure is verified here and none is estimated. The 2,000 sq ft figure matters only as the proportionality denominator — an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% of the primary's size, the fraction state law uses for impact fees at 750 sq ft and above.

> **The ADU impact-fee box.** California law bars any impact fee on an ADU under 750 square feet, and requires proportional charges at 750 square feet or more (Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A)). Bakersfield's published schedule contains no ADU-specific impact-fee statement, so the state rule is the only rule this post can state with confidence. The practical consequence for an 800 sq ft ADU: whatever full fee the city applies to a new dwelling, the ADU pays only its proportion — 40% behind a 2,000 sq ft house — but the full fee itself and the city's ADU treatment still have to come from the city. Note the boundary: connection and capacity charges are not "impact fees" under the statute, and an ADU is not a new residential use for those charges unless built with a new single-family dwelling (§65852.2(f)(2)).

## Three things that can still move the number

**1. The schedule's age.** The latest verified master fee schedule is Resolution No. 056-2024, with fee changes effective July 1, 2024 and labeled for 2024–26. A 2025 or 2026 successor schedule was not located. Every permit figure in this post therefore carries a vintage flag: the method (valuation times 0.0055) may still hold, but the factors may have moved. Confirm the current schedule with the city before budgeting.

**2. The school-fee split — mostly resolved.** Bakersfield's school fee is one number for the typical parcel after all: BCSD's June 2024 report sets the 65/35 split with Kern High, so at the $5.38 state maximum the combined total is $5.38 per sq ft ($4,304 for 800 sq ft), and Greenfield Union's own 2026 study confirms the same 65% share ($3.50) for its territory. Two caveats remain: the Kern High $1.88 is computed from the split rather than stated in a Kern High document of its own, and the rates for the city's other elementary districts (Rosedale, Panama-Buena Vista, and others) were not individually verified. The parcel's districts still must be confirmed first.

**3. The park fee's map.** The only published park figure touching Bakersfield — $3,278 per unit — belongs to the North of the River Recreation and Park District, not to the city as a whole, and it states no ADU treatment. A lot outside the district pays nothing under it; a lot inside it may pay something proportional, or something else entirely. Until the city states the ADU treatment and the lot's district is checked, the park line stays out of the total.
