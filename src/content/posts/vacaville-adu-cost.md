Vacaville is the rare thin page where the rule is clearer than the bill. Ordinance No. 2005, adopted August 12, 2025, writes the 750 sq ft cliff and the proportional formula straight into the municipal code — no bulletin prose, no handout paraphrase. An ADU under 750 sq ft is exempt; an ADU at 750 or above pays the full applicable fee multiplied by the ADU's share of the primary dwelling. The city's 2026 fee sheets then publish every component of that full fee in unusual detail. What they do not publish is the one thing that turns the formula into a number: your main house's square footage. And they do not publish a building permit table at all.

Our six-phase total lands at **$306,000, or $383 per square foot** — a floor, not a total, marked with the asterisk where it appears. It counts the series-baseline construction phases, $14,000 of design, and the city sheet's $4,136 school fee. It excludes the building permit, the proportional impact fees, sewer and water connection charges, and county facilities fees, all marked "verify with city" below. Construction phases 2–6 are carried from the Los Angeles County baseline; Vacaville does not publish phase-level construction costs.

## Three fees homeowners most often get wrong

**1. Adding the component bands into an ADU total.** The parks, traffic, and fire figures above are full single-family fees. An 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft house pays 40% of the applicable band; behind a 1,000 sq ft house, 80%. Summing the bands gives a new-house bill, not an ADU bill, and it is the single most common way Vacaville estimates go wrong. The ordinance's formula is explicit; the inputs are the primary dwelling's size and the band it falls in.

**2. Treating the sewer range as a fixed price.** The sewer charge is genuinely ADU-specific — an EDU factor by primary-dwelling size — but it is still a range: 0.4 EDU for a primary under 1,000 sq ft, up to 0.7 EDU for a primary of 2,500 sq ft or more, multiplied by $7,403 inside the Downtown Specific Plan Benefit area or $9,153 outside it. The range is $2,961 to $6,407. Budgeting either endpoint as "the" sewer fee misstates the line by thousands. Note also a $1 presentation discrepancy between the city's two 2026 sheets ($7,403 vs. $7,405 per EDU) — the same charge shown twice, not two charges.

**3. Assuming the school fee is settled.** The city sheet's $5.17 is dated January 1, 2026 — before the state ceiling rose to $5.38 on January 28, 2026 — and Vacaville's two districts do not share one history. Vacaville Unified's 2024 study references the then-maximum $5.17, but Travis Unified's own 2022–23 report says it collects Level 2 fees, with a stale $6.66 figure as its last published number. A Travis-side parcel at a Level 2 rate would owe materially more than $4,136. The city sheet is the best published figure; the district is the authority. Confirm before budgeting.

## The missing permit table

Vacaville's 2026 sheets are thorough about connection and impact fees and silent about the permit counter itself. The sheets say fees are subject to the schedule in effect at permit issuance and that estimates are available through the Building Division — which is a direction, not a price. No valuation table, flat fee, or plan-check rate for ADUs was located. This is the largest single hole in the Vacaville picture: every other fee line can at least be bounded, but the permit cannot even be estimated from documents. It stays excluded, and the total's asterisk carries it.

Treat any claim of a live Vacaville pre-approved program as unverified until a city document says otherwise.

## A note on SB 1117

Signed September 2026, effective January 1, 2027: for a homeowner's first two ADUs, impact fees may apply only to square footage above 750, calculated marginally. Vacaville's proportional formula cannot survive that math unchanged — an 800 sq ft ADU would owe fees on 50 sq ft of proportional impact, not 800, shrinking the cliff penalty to a sliver of its current size. School fees are outside SB 1117's impact-fee definition and stay at the district's adopted rate. The January 2027 quarterly review will check for an amended Ordinance 2005 and a district-confirmed school rate.
