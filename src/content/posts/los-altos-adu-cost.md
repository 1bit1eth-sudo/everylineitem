Los Altos's $303,712 is a floor with a split school fee: half the school story is verified, half is not. The high-school district posts its rate openly; the elementary district's current rate could not be located. And the city's own FY 2026-27 schedule, unusually, publishes an ADU building permit table whose valuation basis is nowhere in the schedule — so the permit stays uncomputed despite the table's existence.

The counted lines are the **construction tax of $336** ($0.42 per square foot × 800, single-family rate under Municipal Code Chapter 3.24, confirmed in the FY 2026-27 schedule) and the **Mountain View Los Altos High School District fee of $1,376** ($1.72 per square foot × 800, the district's posted residential rate). The districts share the Level I fee 33.33% high school / 66.67% feeder, so the missing elementary share is the larger piece — roughly twice the counted high-school share at any given Level I rate — and it is the single most important gap in this post.

Two near-misses deserve explicit mention. First, the July 2026 MVLA justification study justifies a $1.79 high-school share (33.33% of the $5.38 SAB rate), which would add $56 — but the district's live business page still posts $1.72, so $1.72 is counted and $1.79 is flagged as the adoption variant. Second, the FY 2026-27 schedule lists City Capacity Rights at $6,456.29 under sewer permits, but states no ADU treatment, so it is shown, not counted — counting it without an ADU basis would be guessing.

## The ADU law box

Three state rules frame every number above. Under Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A), a local agency may not impose impact fees on an ADU under 750 square feet; at 750 or above, fees are charged proportionately to the primary dwelling's size. Under §66311.5 (SB 1117, effective January 1, 2027), per-square-foot impact fees on two or fewer ADUs are charged only on the area above 750 square feet — 50 square feet on this 800 sq ft benchmark — and connection and capacity charges are excluded from that rule. School fees are a separate statute: under SB 543, ADUs under 500 square feet pay $0 in school fees, and at 800 square feet the full district rate applies. SB 1117 does not reduce school fees, and it does not change any line counted in this post: the construction tax is a tax, not an impact fee, and the MVLA share is a school fee.

## Three things that can still move the number

1. **The LASD elementary share.** At the 2026 Level I rate the 66.67% share would be about $3.59 per square foot — roughly $2,872 on 800 sq ft — dwarfing everything counted here. A single current LASD posting closes the largest gap in this post.
2. **The ADU permit valuation basis.** The schedule's ADU permit table is unusable without the city's valuation dollars per square foot for an ADU. That one input converts the permit and both plan-check percentages into figures.
3. **Chapter 3.60 impact fees and sewer capacity.** Both exist in city documents without ADU amounts or treatment. Either could add thousands; neither is counted on a guess.

## How Los Altos compares

Only published posts are used here. Published Bay Area totals: Palo Alto **~$303,000**, Fremont **$314,000**, Campbell **~$318,000**, Oakland **$322,000**. Los Altos's $303,712 sits at the low end of that band while missing the larger elementary school share, the permit, impact fees, and sewer charges entirely; once those are counted, expect it to land mid-band or above — the missing LASD share alone moves it most of the way there.
