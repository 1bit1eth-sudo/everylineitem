**DRAFT — not for publication until reviewed.** Numbers below are verified against City of Arcadia and Arcadia Unified documents as of November 13, 2026, unless marked otherwise.

Arcadia ADU fees are a study in what a fee schedule can say without ever naming a price. The city publishes a full valuation-based permit schedule, a pre-approved plan program, and a school district notice with a rate to the cent — and still no single dollar figure for an 800 sq ft ADU permit. An 800 sq ft detached ADU here lands at **$309,000, or $386 per square foot** — a floor, not a total. It counts the series-baseline construction phases, $14,000 of design, and $7,380 in verified fees: school $4,304, park $2,280, fire $280, permit issuing $63, solid waste $7, water meter $159, and backflow inspection $287. Only the valuation-based building permit fee and its percentage-based plan check are excluded, each for a stated reason, and each marked "verify with city" below.

One line is deliberately absent. A verified floor total for a 2,000 sq ft single-family new construction cannot be stated from official sources: fee schedules price permits and fees, not construction, so no such figure is verified here and none is estimated. The 2,000 sq ft figure matters only as the proportionality denominator — an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft primary dwelling is 40% of the primary's size, the fraction the city's own ADU standards use for impact fees at 750 sq ft and above.

> **The ADU impact-fee box.** California law bars any impact fee on an ADU under 750 square feet, and requires proportional charges at 750 square feet or more (Government Code §65852.2(f)(3)(A)). Arcadia's own ADU standards say the same thing and give the formula: ADU floor area divided by primary dwelling floor area, times the typical fee for a new dwelling. For an 800 sq ft ADU behind a 2,000 sq ft house, the fraction is 40% — and because both typical fees are published as per-square-foot rates, the formula now produces dollars: park $2.85 × 800 = $2,280 and fire $0.35 × 800 = $280, both counted in this post's total. (For a per-square-foot fee, the proportional rule equals rate × ADU area.) Note the boundary of the rule: the city's definition of "impact fee" covers Mitigation Fee Act and Quimby Act fees, not water or sewer connection fees or capacity charges, which are treated separately.

## Three things that can still move the number

**1. The permit's valuation base.** The schedule's percentages are exact — 65% plan check, 20% energy review, 5.5% technology — and exactly useless without the base. The valuation table lists dwelling rates from $154.24 to $164.40 per sq ft by construction type, but nothing in the material found says which type an 800 sq ft detached ADU takes. Picking a type would be guessing, so the whole permit line stays out of the total until the city prices it.

**2. The school fee's adoption paper trail.** The $5.38 rate comes from the district's hearing notice, which states the proposed rate and the April 27, 2026 effective date (60 days after the February 24, 2026 hearing). The notice is a proposal document: it says the fee applies "if adopted." The site's fee data records this rate as verified, but the adoption resolution itself was not re-opened in this session. Confirm adoption with the district before carrying $4,304 into a budget — and confirm the parcel is actually in Arcadia Unified.

**3. The water connection is scenario-dependent.** The meter ($159) and backflow inspection ($287) are verified and counted here because this post prices a new detached ADU, which needs a new, separate water service under the city's ADU standards. A converted ADU needs no new connection and pays $0 for both — so the $446 in this post's total drops to $0 on a conversion.
