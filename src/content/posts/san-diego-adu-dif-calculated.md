There is no single San Diego ADU impact-fee number, and any article that gives
you one without asking how many ADUs are on the lot is guessing. Our own city
post carries a $5,000–$8,000 range for newly triggered impact fees — and labels
it, honestly, as an estimate, not a rate-table figure. This post replaces that
estimate with the mechanism: the ordinance text that decides whether you owe
anything at all, the scaling table that prices what you owe, and the two worked
comparisons that show why the same 800 sq ft ADU can owe two different amounts
on two different lots.

The governing text is San Diego Municipal Code §142.0640, not Information
Bulletin 501. IB-501 is the fee schedule for construction permits — plan check
and inspection — and it is a separate bill. DIF is the infrastructure charge
for parks, mobility, library, and fire-rescue facilities, set by City Council
resolution by community, and §142.0640 is the section that says who pays it.

## The short answer

| Step | Rule (SDMC §142.0640) |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Exempt? | First two ADUs on a premises: exempt at any size. Any ADU under 750 sq ft gross floor area: exempt. Owner-occupancy affidavit (3 years): exempt at any size. |
| 2. If not exempt | Multiple-dwelling-unit rate, scaled by Table 142-06A **or** proportional to the primary dwelling — whichever is **lower**. |
| 3. Cap | Never more than the DIF for the primary dwelling on the same lot. |
| When due | Before final inspection; the amount is the lower of the schedule in effect when the application was deemed complete or when the fees are paid, plus the automatic annual July 1 increase. |

## Step 1 — The exemptions, in the order to check them

**The first-two rule is the one most guides miss.** §142.0640(b)(1)(A) exempts
the first two ADUs on a premises from DIF "regardless of the gross floor area
of the Accessory Dwelling Unit." A 1,200 sq ft first ADU owes $0 in DIF. Two
exceptions: ADUs built under the Bonus Program (§143.1305(c)(1)) pay DIF when
they exceed 750 sq ft, and when more than two ADUs are proposed, the exemption
applies to the two smallest — the third unit, and any after it, is where DIF
starts.

**The under-750 rule.** ADUs "less than 750 square feet in gross floor area"
are exempt, as are Junior ADUs, movable tiny houses, and guest quarters. Note
the wording carefully: the ordinance says *less than* 750 in *gross floor
area*, while state law exempts ADUs of 750 sq ft or less of *interior livable
space*. Those are two different measures. A unit sitting exactly on the line —
750 sq ft gross floor area — is not exempt under the ordinance's "less than"
clause, though it may still be exempt as one of the first two ADUs or under
state law if its interior livable space is 750 or less. If your drawings land
on 750 exactly, get the city's calculation in writing before you budget $0.

**The owner-occupancy rule.** An ADU of any size is exempt when the record
owner agrees to live in one of the dwelling units as a primary residence for at
least three years from building permit issuance, backed by a recorded
affidavit signed before the permit issues. Community land trusts and qualified
nonprofit corporations are excepted from the affidavit requirement.

If none of these covers your unit, you move to step 2 — which, for a typical
single-family lot building its first ADU, never happens.

## Step 2 — The two calculations, and why the lower one wins

§142.0640(b)(1)(B) prices a fee-owing ADU at "the multiple dwelling unit
rate," then applies the lower of two methods:

**Method A — size-scaled.** Table 142-06A scales the full multiple-dwelling-unit
rate by unit size. The ADU-relevant slice:

| ADU size | Scaled rate |
| --- | --- |
| 751–800 sq ft | 78% of the MDU rate |
| 801–850 sq ft | 80% |
| 851–900 sq ft | 83% |
| 901–950 sq ft | 85% |
| 951–1,000 sq ft | 87% |
| 1,001–1,050 sq ft | 90% |
| 1,051–1,100 sq ft | 92% |
| 1,101–1,150 sq ft | 95% |
| 1,151–1,200 sq ft | 97% |
| 1,201–1,250 sq ft | 99% |
| 1,251 sq ft and up | Full fee |

**Method B — proportional.** The ADU's square footage divided by the primary
dwelling's square footage, times the multiple-dwelling-unit rate. An 800 sq ft
ADU behind a 1,600 sq ft house is 50% of the rate; behind an 800 sq ft house it
is 100%.

The ordinance takes whichever is lower, and caps the result at the primary
dwelling's own DIF. Two comparisons show why that matters:

| Case: 800 sq ft third ADU | Method A (scaled) | Method B (proportional) | Fee owed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary dwelling 1,600 sq ft | 78% of MDU rate | 800 ÷ 1,600 = 50% of MDU rate | **50%** — proportional wins |
| Primary dwelling 800 sq ft | 78% of MDU rate | 800 ÷ 800 = 100% of MDU rate | **78%** — scaled wins |

Same ADU, same ordinance, a 28-percentage-point swing decided entirely by the
size of the existing house. No flat range can capture that — which is why the
$5,000–$8,000 estimate in our city post should be read as a placeholder for
this formula, not as a quote.

## What this post deliberately does not give you: a dollar total

The per-unit multiple-dwelling-unit rate is set by City Council resolution and
varies by community — San Diego runs both DIF communities and Facilities
Benefit Assessment (FBA) communities, each with its own schedule, and the four
citywide components (parks, mobility, library, fire-rescue) sit on top of that
structure under Build Better SD. We checked for a single current citywide
per-unit rate table that would let us turn the percentages above into dollars
and did not find one we could cite to an official source: the published
planning-department schedule we could verify is from FY 2024, and a July 2026
"reform" resolution number circulating on builder blogs could not be confirmed
against a city source. So this post publishes the formula and the percentages
— both verifiable in the ordinance text — and no dollar total. That does not
leave a reader with nothing: IB-501 §III.8 points to the city's Citywide DIF
Calculator on City Planning's Public Spaces page, where entering the parcel
number and development details returns a project-specific estimate. What the
calculator does not give us is a citable published rate table — which is why
our ledger entry FI-004 (the $5,000–$8,000 impact-fee range) stays marked
unverified until a current community rate table is confirmed; it is not
replaced by a new estimate.

## What DIF is not

- **Building permit fees (IB-501).** Plan check and inspection, charged on
  every ADU. Our city post cites $8,085.26 plan check plus $8,401.90 inspection
  for an 800 sq ft detached ADU in the City of San Diego under IB-501 — that
  is the permit bill, not DIF, and it applies whether or not DIF is $0.
- **School fees.** A separate district charge with its own threshold: exempt
  under 500 sq ft, charged above it. Crossing 750 changes DIF, not the school
  fee's existence.
- **Water and sewer connection or capacity charges.** Separate utility
  charges with separate exemption rules.
- **The Bonus Program Community Enhancement Fee.** A separate fee on Bonus
  Program ADUs, which are the same units that lose the first-two DIF exemption
  above 750 sq ft. Do not confuse it with DIF.

## Coming January 1, 2027 — SB 1117

From January 1, 2027, state law (Gov. Code §66311.5, as amended by SB 1117)
narrows impact fees on a homeowner's first two ADUs to the square footage
*above* 750 — an 800 sq ft ADU would owe fees on 50 sq ft, not 800. Because
San Diego already exempts the first two ADUs entirely, the practical effect in
the city falls mainly on third-and-later ADUs and Bonus Program units, and how
the 2027 marginal rule interacts with the local scaling table for those units
will depend on the city's 2027 schedule. The math above reflects 2026 rules.
See [SB 1117 kills the 750 sq ft cliff](/posts/sb-1117-kills-750-sq-ft-cliff/)
for the statewide change, and our [San Diego city post](/posts/san-diego-adu-cost/)
for the full build cost this fee sits inside.

## Method and limits

- Ordinance text was read from the Municode publication of SDMC §142.0640 on
  3 Nov 2026; the scaling table is quoted from 751 sq ft up. Bands below 751 sq ft
  are omitted because a fee-owing ADU below 751 sq ft can only occur at exactly
  750 sq ft — a third-or-later unit, which owes DIF at "750 or more" — at 76%.
- No community per-unit MDU rate is published here because no current official
  table was verified in this check; the FY 2024 planning schedule is stale and
  a builder-blog "Resolution 2026-34" claim was not confirmed against a city
  source. A reader who needs a dollar figure should pull the DIF/FBA schedule
  for their own planning area and apply the formula above — or ask Development
  Services for the calculation in writing.
- For DIF communities, DIF amounts rise automatically each July 1 by the
  Construction Cost Index (§142.0640(c)); FBA-community schedules follow their
  own adopted fee schedules. Re-check before budgeting.
